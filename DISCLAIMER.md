# Disclaimer

This is an independent, non-accredited theological education project. It is modeled on publicly described course titles, catalog descriptions, degree requirements, and academic progression associated with Wheaton College, Gordon-Conwell Theological Seminary, and Princeton Theological Seminary.

It is not produced, approved, taught, accredited, sponsored, or endorsed by those institutions. Institutional names and catalog links are used only to identify source metadata. All independently generated lectures, syllabi, schedules, assignments, examinations, rubrics, and study materials must be labeled accordingly.

Completion of this curriculum does not confer academic credit, admission eligibility, ordination, licensure, certification, or a degree. Human mentoring and evaluation are incorporated where possible but do not convert the project into an accredited program.

Nothing in this repository is professional legal, medical, mental-health, financial, or safeguarding advice. Pastoral-care exercises must respect competence, confidentiality, mandatory-reporting duties, and referral boundaries.
