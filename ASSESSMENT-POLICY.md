# Assessment and Grading Policy

## Grading scale

| Grade | Range | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| A | 93–100 | Excellent mastery |
| A- | 90–92 | Very strong mastery |
| B+ | 87–89 | Strong mastery |
| B | 83–86 | Graduate progression threshold |
| B- | 80–82 | Below normal graduate progression threshold |
| C+ | 77–79 | Adequate undergraduate work |
| C | 73–76 | Undergraduate progression threshold |
| C- | 70–72 | Below undergraduate progression threshold |
| D | 60–69 | Deficient |
| F | Below 60 | Failure |

A course may adopt a stricter scale when justified and documented before instruction begins.

## Normal undergraduate assessment pattern

- Weekly reading notes or responses.
- Retrieval quizzes.
- Short analytical papers.
- Discussion or oral response.
- Midterm examination.
- Major research paper or project.
- Final examination or cumulative project.
- Course portfolio.

## Normal MDiv assessment pattern

- Exegesis papers.
- Greek and Hebrew translation/parsing.
- Theological essays.
- Sermon manuscripts and recorded sermons.
- Ministry case studies and reflections.
- Oral examinations.
- Research papers.
- Mentored-ministry evaluation.

## Normal PhD assessment pattern

- Critical reviews and review essays.
- Annotated bibliographies.
- Historiographical and methodological essays.
- Seminar papers suitable for conference revision.
- Language examinations.
- Comprehensive examinations.
- Dissertation prospectus.
- Chapter submissions.
- Oral defense preparation.

At least two doctoral seminar papers should be revised into conference-style papers. Dissertation work must use relevant original-language primary sources.

## Exam modes

Courses may use closed-book, open-book, take-home, oral, or mixed examinations. Language and comprehensive examinations may be timed. Oral examinations and sermons should be recorded where practical.

## Retake policy

One structured retake may be offered after required remediation. The course record must retain the original result, remediation, retake result, and final progression decision.

## Rubric requirements

Every graded assignment must map to one or more course learning outcomes and have a rubric or answer-key standard. Detailed grading explanations belong in `instructor-materials/`.

## Human sign-off

Milestones requiring human judgment remain incomplete until the designated evaluator signs the completion record. This includes preaching delivery, pastoral simulations, advanced pronunciation when required, senior capstone review, graduate comprehensive examinations, and dissertation approval/defense.
