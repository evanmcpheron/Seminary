# Dissertation

The dissertation topic remains open until the doctoral stage and must emerge from coursework, language competence, primary-source research, and human supervision. This repository may support research management and critique, but AI may not author dissertation prose for submission as the student's own work.

Required completion claims include a qualified human supervisor, at least two additional readers, original-language primary-source work where relevant, comprehensive examinations, approved prospectus, chapter review, oral defense, and final revision.
