# Decision Log

## ADR-0001 — Project identity

The project is independent and non-accredited. Institutional names remain only as catalog-source metadata.

## ADR-0002 — Confessional framework

The curriculum is broadly evangelical and Protestant, affirms biblical inerrancy and the 66-book Protestant canon, and uses a complementarian constructive default for pastoral office while treating alternative Protestant arguments accurately.

## ADR-0003 — Curriculum sequencing

All spreadsheet courses are preserved. Undergraduate courses may be reassigned within the four-year stage to satisfy prerequisites. Overloads may be redistributed to summer terms.

## ADR-0004 — Writing style

Canonical files use Markdown and YAML. Student papers use SBL with Chicago notes-and-bibliography conventions and Turabian-compatible formatting.

## ADR-0005 — Production scope

The default production unit is one course design or one course-week. Semester-wide scaffolds are permitted; semester-wide full instructional content in one pass is not.

## ADR-0006 — Human evaluation

Routine external mentoring is deferred during years 1–3. It becomes strongly recommended for the senior capstone and required for MDiv ministry formation and PhD supervision/defense.
