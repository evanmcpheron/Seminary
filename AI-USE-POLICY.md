# AI Use Policy

## Curriculum development

AI-generated lectures, bibliographies, questions, assignments, rubrics, and answer keys are drafts until independently checked against verified sources, the project constitution, and the relevant audit prompts.

Every generation run must create a provenance record containing:

- Active prompt.
- Scope.
- Model/tool identity when available.
- Input files read.
- Files written.
- Sources consulted.
- Validation commands and outcomes.
- Unresolved `TODO-VERIFY` items.
- Human approvals required.

## Student work

AI may:

- Clarify assignment instructions.
- Explain concepts without producing the submitted answer.
- Generate ungraded practice questions.
- Identify grammar or organization problems after the student has produced a genuine draft.
- Provide feedback using the published rubric.
- Help locate possible sources that the student then verifies and reads.

AI may not:

- Draft graded essays or research papers for submission.
- Write sermon manuscripts for submission.
- Perform translations or parsing submitted as the student's own work.
- Answer graded quizzes, exams, oral exams, or comprehensive exams.
- Write prospectus or dissertation prose for submission.
- Fabricate or repair citations without source verification.
- Conceal AI assistance.

Every major assignment includes an AI-use declaration. Git history may be used to verify the writing process. Practice quizzes must be clearly separated from graded assessments.
