## Term labels

- `Y1S1` = Year 1, Fall semester
- `Y1S2` = Year 1, Spring semester
- `Y1SU` = Year 1, Summer
- `Y5J` = Year 5, January term
- The same pattern continues for later years.

Rows within the same term are concurrent courses; their order below preserves the curriculum manifest/source ordering after grouping by the reconciled planned term.

The table includes all 68 canonical undergraduate/MDiv courses plus the 18 PhD seminar/colloquium instructional requirements. Non-class doctoral requirements such as language competencies, examinations, program reviews, dissertation work, proposal milestones, and defense milestones are intentionally excluded.
