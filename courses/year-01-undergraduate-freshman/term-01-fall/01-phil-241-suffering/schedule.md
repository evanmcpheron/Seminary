# Course Schedule and Workload

Weeks 1–14 are instructional weeks with two substantial written lectures each.
Week 15 contains no new instruction and is reserved for final synthesis and the
cumulative final examination. Lecture labels below are design-level titles, not
outlines or lecture content. Named anthology readings identify verified authors
or source groupings; exact pagination is intentionally omitted unless checked
against the student's acquired edition.

## Workload interpretation

- **Written lectures — 42 hours:** two substantial lectures in each of weeks
  1–14, budgeted at 1.5 hours of study per lecture.
- **Required reading and source study — 56 hours:** four hours in each
  instructional week, including close reading, comparison, and source annotation.
- **Guided argument practice and discussion — 28 hours:** reconstruction,
  objection testing, biblical-text comparison, discussion, and pastoral-case
  practice. Later prompts must supply the actual activities.
- **Graded assessment work — 54 hours:** the exact assessment-hour allocation in
  [assessment-map.md](assessment-map.md), including final preparation.

The total is exactly `4 credits × 45 hours = 180 hours`, or an average of 12
hours across each of the 15 weeks. Later production may redistribute work within
a week only if the course total, assessment allocations, and approved sequence
remain intact or are formally revised and revalidated.
