# Instructor Grading Guide — Reading and Argument Journal 1

Use the published [rubric](../rubric.md). This guide supplies accuracy anchors,
not a model response for student submission.

## Acceptable source-claim families

A student may select another genuine claim, but common defensible selections
include:

- Lewis chapter 1: Christian claims about righteous ultimate reality create a
  distinct intellectual problem from the fact of pain; Lewis's staged account
  moves through numinous and moral awareness to a Christian historical claim.
- Lewis chapter 2: omnipotence includes all coherent possibilities but not
  contradictions; distinct embodied agents act within a relatively stable
  common order.
- Peterson's introduction: evil pressures belief in a supremely powerful,
  intelligent/knowing, and good God; logical, evidential, and existential
  versions require distinct analysis and response strategies.

Verify the student's formulation against the lawful source copy. Do not accept
a lecture summary mislabeled as the author's exact claim.

## Objection anchors

A strong objection should target a premise, inference, definition, or scope.
Examples of legitimate pressure include whether Lewis's historical sequence is
established, whether a stable order requires evils of the actual kinds or scale,
whether an appeal to coherent possibility answers evidential rather than merely
logical pressure, or whether a classification obscures mixed causes. These are
directions for evaluating relevance, not prose to give the student.

## Pastoral-judgment anchors

Full credit requires a real distinction between classroom and first-response
use. It is not enough to append “be sensitive.” The student should identify a
feature such as consent, timing, safety, role, epistemic limits, or the
difference between general doctrine and a claim about a particular loss.

## Scoring procedure

1. Score each criterion independently; fractional points to one decimal are
   permitted.
2. Record one accuracy strength and one next step.
3. Do not reward confessional agreement or penalize responsible disagreement.
4. If a source location appears fabricated, pause grading and apply the course
   academic-integrity process. Do not invent or repair the citation.
5. If prohibited AI authorship is suspected, request process evidence and
   preserve the original submission. Do not make an unsupported accusation.

The raw score is out of 10. After all twelve entries, average the journal scores
to calculate A-01, which is 10 percent of the course grade.
