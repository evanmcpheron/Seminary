# Week 1 — Framing Suffering and Evil

## Weekly purpose

Week 1 supplies the conceptual and practical grammar for the course. The student
distinguishes major kinds and formulations of evil, reads Lewis's opening
account alongside Peterson's orientation to the field, represents a serious
skeptical challenge charitably, and learns why philosophical explanation and
pastoral response require different standards and timing.

No personal disclosure is required. Case work uses synthetic examples and
observes the privacy, safety, competence, and referral limits in the course
policies.
