## Score interpretation

- 9.3–10.0: A-range performance
- 8.3–9.2: B-range performance
- 7.3–8.2: C-range performance
- 6.0–7.2: D-range performance
- below 6.0: F-range performance

The journal is scored for accurate, charitable analysis and bounded judgment,
not for agreement with the course's confessional conclusion. Fabricated
citations or concealed prohibited AI authorship must be handled under the course
academic-integrity policy rather than quietly repaired by the evaluator.
