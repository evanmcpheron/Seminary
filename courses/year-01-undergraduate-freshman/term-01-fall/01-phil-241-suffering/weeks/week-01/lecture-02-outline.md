# Lecture 2 Outline — Four Kinds of Question

## Objectives

- Differentiate philosophical, theological, existential, and pastoral inquiry.
- Match a response to the question actually being asked.
- Present skeptical and Christian perspectives without caricature.
- Apply trauma-aware boundaries to a synthetic case.

## Outline

1. One reality, several questions
2. Philosophical inquiry: consistency, evidence, and explanation
3. Theological inquiry: God, creation, sin, providence, Christ, and hope
4. Existential inquiry: protest, trust, identity, and meaning
5. Pastoral inquiry: presence, agency, safety, and competent care
6. A four-lens case analysis
7. Failure modes: category mistakes, premature explanation, and blame
8. Confessional synthesis: truth, lament, love, and epistemic humility

## Key terms

Philosophy of religion; theology; existential problem; pastoral response;
speech act; explanation; protest; lament; trauma-aware; non-coercive; referral;
safeguarding; epistemic humility.
