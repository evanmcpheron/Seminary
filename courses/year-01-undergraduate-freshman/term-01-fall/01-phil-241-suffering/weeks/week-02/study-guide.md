# Week 2 Study Guide — Text, Inference, and Canonical Pattern

Complete this guide after the readings and lectures. It is ungraded guided
practice. Use the assigned texts and your own notes. Do not record personal
trauma or another person's private information.

## 1. Genesis claim/inference sort — 15 minutes

For each statement, label it **direct textual claim**, **responsible theological
inference**, or **unsupported case-specific claim**. Cite the verse or lecture
section that controls your judgment.

1. God evaluates the completed creation as very good.
2. Creaturely moral limits exist only after human sin.
3. Genesis 3 narrates mistrust, transgression, shame, blame, judgment, and exile.
4. Every person's severe pain is punishment for that person's severe sin.
5. The fall framework relates present disorder to creaturely rebellion.
6. Genesis 3 by itself supplies a complete account of animal suffering and
   geological history.

Revise each unsupported statement so that it makes no claim beyond the evidence.

## 5. Canonical synthesis and error log — 10 minutes

Draw a six-node sequence using these headings: **creation**, **rebellion and
disorder**, **lament**, **providence and creaturely limits**, **groaning and
inseparable love**, **judgment and new creation**. Under each node, cite one
assigned passage and write one claim the passage supports.

Finish with two corrections:

> I first thought ____. The assigned text requires the more precise claim ____
> because ____.

Retain the map and error log for cumulative quiz and examination review. Do not
submit this guide for a grade.
