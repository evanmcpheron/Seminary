# Instructor Guide — A-03 Development 1

## Scope

This is a required 1.5-hour process milestone within A-03, not a separately
graded assignment. Its purpose is to reveal conceptual and source-control gaps
before the student encounters the later primary argument. Do not score this
artifact or treat the provisional map as the final A-03 submission.

## Completion check

Mark complete only when all seven student-facing headings are present and the
work contains:

- corrected definitions of logical/evidential problem, validity, soundness,
  defense, theodicy, and apparently gratuitous evil;
- a provisional logical or evidential family choice explicitly open to revision;
- a numbered 4–6-premise diagnostic map and conclusion;
- visible marks on premises needing a primary source or clarification;
- one ambiguity, one suppressed premise, and one serious current objection;
- accurate labeling of the Week 1 lecture map as curriculum synthesis; and
- sources actually consulted plus an AI-use declaration.

If a component is absent, return the sheet with only the missing element named.
Do not add the premise, source, objection, or wording for the student.

## Feedback prompts

Use questions such as:

- “Which term changes meaning between these two premises?”
- “What connecting premise would be required for the conclusion to follow?”
- “Which later primary source must authorize this attribution?”
- “Does this objection deny a premise, challenge an inference, or introduce
  counterevidence?”
- “What would make the opposing view recognizable to its defender?”

Avoid prescribing which argument family the student must finally select.

## Integrity boundary

AI may explain a named term or critique a student-created map. It may not create,
repair, or rewrite the map or objection. If the declaration or process history
raises a concern, use the course integrity process. Do not infer a violation
merely because the map is polished.

## Carry-forward note

Retain a minimal completion record and the student's own list of gaps. Week 3
may add historical-source control, and Week 4 will provide the final
source-specific completion stage. Do not generate or preview those later-week
materials here.
