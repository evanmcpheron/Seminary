# Instructor Grading Guide — A-01 Journal 2

Use the student-facing rubric in `../rubric.md`. Grade the student's source
control and reasoning, not agreement with the curriculum's confessional
conclusions.

## Source checks

The selected claim must be locatable in one of the assigned sources. Acceptable
biblical locations are Genesis 1–3; Job 1–2 or 38–42; Psalms 22, 73, or 88;
Romans 8:18–39; and Revelation 21–22. Lewis work must identify chapter 3,
“Divine Goodness,” in the student's lawful edition.

For biblical entries, verify that both ESV 2025 and NASB 2020 are identified.
The student may responsibly report that no observed wording difference changed
the interpretation. Do not reward an invented Greek or Hebrew claim, a claim
about translation philosophy unsupported by the assigned records, or a
doctrinal inference presented as though both English versions stated it
verbatim.

## Content anchors

Strong entries generally do four things:

- locate a narrow claim rather than attempt the entire problem of suffering;
- trace actual narrative, poetic, or argumentative support;
- distinguish objection to an inference from denial that the source says what
  it says; and
- identify a concrete pastoral misuse, such as victim-blaming, hidden-purpose
  claims, coerced lament, proof-texting Romans 8:28, treating persons as
  replaceable, or using perfecting love to excuse abuse.

An entry need not reach the lecture's confessional conclusion. A skeptical
evaluation may receive full credit when the source is represented accurately,
the objection targets a real inference, and the pastoral judgment is
responsible.

## Common scoring errors

- Do not award “claim and support” credit for accumulating verses without
  explaining their relation.
- Do not deduct for avoiding a direct quotation; paraphrase is preferred.
- Do not treat emotional reserve or intensity as evidence of spiritual quality.
- Do not turn a missing AI declaration into a guessed authorship judgment;
  follow the published integrity procedure.
- Do not repair an invented citation on the student's behalf. Flag it and use
  the academic-integrity process where warranted.

## Feedback boundary

Give one source-control comment and one reasoning or pastoral-judgment comment.
Feedback may point to a lecture heading or verse range. It must not rewrite the
entry or supply prose for a resubmission.
