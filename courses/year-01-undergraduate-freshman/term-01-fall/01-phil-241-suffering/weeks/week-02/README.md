# Week 2 — Biblical Grammar for Suffering

## Weekly purpose

Week 2 traces the canonical pattern of good creation, creaturely rebellion,
lament, providence, judgment, and new-creation hope. The student compares the
assigned ESV 2025 and NASB 2020 passages, reconstructs Lewis's account of divine
goodness, and practices distinguishing textual claims, theological inferences,
and unwarranted diagnoses of particular suffering.

No personal disclosure is required. The discussion uses a fictional case and
observes the privacy, safety, competence, and referral limits in the course
policies.
