# Week 2 Assignments

This file contains the two Week 2 written tasks: the graded A-01 journal entry
and the required A-03 development milestone. Complete both only after the
assigned readings and the two audited lectures.

## A-01-J02 — Reading and Argument Journal 2

**Parent assessment:** A-01 Reading and argument journals

**Status:** graded, 10 points within the twelve-entry A-01 series

**Time budget:** 0.50 hour

**Outcomes:** `LO-01`, `LO-04`, `LO-07`

### Purpose

Demonstrate accurate source use, critical evaluation, and responsible
distinction between theological interpretation and pastoral application.

### Task

Choose one focused claim from **one** of these sources:

- Genesis 1–3;
- Job 1–2 or 38–42;
- Psalm 22, 73, or 88;
- Romans 8:18–39;
- Revelation 21–22; or
- Lewis, *The Problem of Pain*, chapter 3.

Write **300–400 words** under four headings:

1. **Claim:** State the source's claim accurately. For a biblical text, identify
   the passage's claim before stating a later canonical or theological
   inference.
2. **Reasons or textual movement:** Explain at least two reasons, narrative
   features, or stages that support the claim in its immediate context.
3. **Objection or limit:** State a serious objection to the inference, or explain
   a limit on what the source establishes. Represent a critic charitably.
4. **Significance:** Explain why the claim matters for Christian thinking about
   suffering, then give one sentence distinguishing responsible classroom use
   from a possible pastoral misuse.

Use paraphrase. A direct quotation is not required. If you quote, keep it brief
and cite the exact verse, page, or licensed digital location. If selecting
Scripture, consult both assigned translations and note one meaningful wording
difference or state that no difference altered your interpretation. Do not make
an original-language claim.

End with a short-form source entry that makes the selected passage locatable.
Submit UTF-8 Markdown with this metadata:

If Lewis is selected, replace `bible_translations_used` with
`source_edition_used` and identify the lawful edition. The word count excludes
metadata and the source entry. Use the notes already made during the reading
block so the work remains within 30 minutes.

### Evaluation and AI-use declaration

See the A-01-J02 section of [rubric.md](rubric.md). The twelve A-01 entries are
averaged and together constitute 10 percent of the course grade.

The substantive entry must be the student's own. AI may clarify instructions or
critique a genuine student draft; it may not select the passage, identify the
claim, reconstruct the reasons, formulate the objection, or draft/rewrite prose
for submission. State “No AI used” or describe the permitted use and affected
portion.

## A-03-D01 — Argument Reconstruction Brief Development 1

**Parent assessment:** A-03 Argument reconstruction brief

**Status:** required development milestone; no separate Week 2 points

**Time budget:** 1.50 hours of the approved five-hour A-03 budget

**Outcome developed this week:** `LO-01`

This milestone builds a provisional argument-control sheet. It is not the final
brief and does not ask you to anticipate sources assigned in Weeks 3–4. The
instructor checks timely completion and process evidence; the finished A-03 is
graded under its later prompt.

### Time-boxed process

| Step | Deliverable | Time |
|---|---|---:|
| Retrieve terms | Define logical problem, evidential problem, validity, soundness, defense, theodicy, and apparently gratuitous evil from memory; then correct from Week 1 | 0.25 h |
| Select a provisional family | Choose **logical** or **evidential** as the candidate argument family and write why the choice remains revisable after later primary sources | 0.25 h |
| Build a diagnostic skeleton | From the introductory skeptical challenge in the Week 1 lectures, draft a numbered 4–6-premise map and conclusion; mark every premise that needs a primary source or clarification | 0.50 h |
| Stress-test | Identify one ambiguity, one suppressed premise, and the strongest current objection; do not attempt a final evaluation | 0.25 h |
| Preserve process evidence | Save the sheet, list only sources actually consulted, and complete the AI-use declaration | 0.25 h |
|  | **Total** | **1.50 h** |

### Required headings

1. Provisional argument family
2. Corrected term-control list
3. Diagnostic premise map
4. Source and clarification gaps
5. Strongest current objection
6. Revision note for Weeks 3–4
7. AI-use declaration

Do not invent an author, quotation, page, or formal premise. Label the Week 1
lecture map as a curriculum synthesis rather than a primary-source argument.
Later work must replace or substantiate the diagnostic skeleton with the exact
assigned primary source before A-03 completion.

AI may explain a named logical term or critique a map the student already made.
It may not construct, repair, or rewrite the submitted map or objection. Keep a
brief record of any permitted assistance.
