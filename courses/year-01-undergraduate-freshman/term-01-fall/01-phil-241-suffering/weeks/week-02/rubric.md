| Criterion | Outcome | Excellent | Proficient | Developing | Deficient | Points |

## A-03-D01 — Development milestone completion standard

This Week 2 milestone receives no separate points and must not be averaged into
the final A-03 score. It is complete when the submitted process sheet contains:

- all seven required headings;
- corrected definitions rather than unreviewed first retrieval;
- a clearly provisional logical or evidential family choice;
- a numbered diagnostic map with unsupported premises visibly marked;
- one ambiguity, one suppressed premise, and one serious objection;
- no invented primary-source attribution, quotation, or page; and
- a complete AI-use declaration.

Incomplete elements are returned for completion. Instructor feedback should
identify process or source-control needs without constructing the student's
final argument.

## Integrity control

Fabricated citations, concealed prohibited AI authorship, use of unauthorized
resources on the closed-book quiz, or other integrity concerns are handled under
the course policy rather than quietly repaired through scoring. Journal and
quiz grading evaluates demonstrated knowledge and reasoning, not assent to the
curriculum's confessional conclusions.
