# Guided Discussion — Which Hypothesis Predicts the Pattern?

This is ungraded practice. Budget 24 minutes. Complete it with a partner when
available or record a structured self-dialogue. Do not use personal trauma,
graphic examples, or a real person's crisis as evidence.

## Case packet

Use this fictional, nonclinical pattern:

- a sentient population has warning pain that usually prompts escape from
  damaging conditions;
- the same warning system extends to members who are not moral agents;
- some token pains occur when escape is no longer possible;
- some pain results from system malfunction;
- pleasure sometimes supports biological goals and sometimes occurs without
  such a contribution; and
- moral agents sometimes respond compassionately and sometimes exploit the
  vulnerability.

The packet stipulates only these features. Do not add a cause, divine purpose,
diagnosis, or numerical frequency.

## Roles and timing

### Round 1 — Draper advocate, 6 minutes

Explain why the pattern is claimed to be less surprising on HI than on theism.
Use O1, O2, O3, biologically appropriate, and pathological accurately. State a
likelihood conclusion, not a posterior verdict.

### Round 2 — Theistic respondent, 6 minutes

Choose one response: embodied creaturely goods, stable order, free will,
Christian canonical expansion, cognitive limitation, or independent
counterevidence. State whether it changes P(O given theism), challenges the
comparison, or affects total evidence. Identify one burden the response leaves
unmet.

### Round 3 — Draper reply, 5 minutes

Present the strongest reply to the selected response. Test independent support,
alternative means, O2 reach, O3 fit, or double counting. Do not claim that the
response fails merely because it is theological.

### Round 4 — Auditor, 5 minutes

Use the seven-step audit from Lecture 2. Identify one accurate claim by each
side, one overstatement, and the strongest conclusion currently warranted.

### Exit reflection, 2 minutes

Record:

1. one reason the evidence favors HI, if it does;
2. one reason the comparison remains uncertain;
3. one difference between Rowe and Draper; and
4. one immediate human duty that is unchanged by the debate.

## Boundaries

Charity requires each role to present the rival at its strongest responsible
level. Confessional disagreement is not a scoring defect, and skeptical
disagreement is not a character defect. If a real disclosure or safety concern
arises, end the exercise and follow appropriate safeguarding, emergency,
medical, mental-health, legal, or pastoral procedures.
