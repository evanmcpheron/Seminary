## A-01-J08 — Reading and Argument Journal 8

**Parent assessment:** A-01 Reading and argument journals

**Status:** graded, 10 points within the twelve-entry A-01 series

**Time budget:** 0.50 hour

**Outcomes:** LO-01, LO-03, LO-05

### Purpose

Explain how Draper's distributional likelihood argument creates an evidential
problem for theism, then evaluate one serious response without confusing local
evidence with a total-evidence verdict.

### Task

Write **300–400 words** under five headings:

1. **Hypotheses and evidence:** State Draper's theism, HI, and O concisely.
   Explain one way HI is narrower than atheism or broader than naturalism.
2. **Distributional reason:** Select O1, O2, or O3. State Draper's comparative
   probability claim and reconstruct the strongest supporting reason. Cite
   Peterson chapter 31 by page or source heading.
3. **Probability role:** Distinguish the likelihood claim from a prior and a
   posterior judgment. State one stronger conclusion the likelihood alone does
   not establish.
4. **Charitable response:** Present one serious theistic expansion,
   comparison-class objection, cognitive-limit response, or total-evidence
   response. Identify whether it changes the likelihood, undercuts the
   comparison, or supplies counterevidence, and state one remaining burden.
5. **Provisional judgment:** Give the strongest conclusion warranted by the
   exchange. Add one sentence explaining why biological function does not
   identify God's purpose for a token suffering and one sentence recognizing
   how a responsible holder of the opposing position could reason.

Use paraphrase. A direct quotation is not required. If one is used, keep it
brief and cite the exact anthology page. Do not research a real tragedy,
compile graphic examples, invent a probability, or assign a hidden divine
purpose.
