# Week 9 — Probabilistic Arguments and Explanatory Comparison

## Weekly purpose

Week 9 moves from Rowe's inference about apparently gratuitous suffering to
Paul Draper's comparison of rival hypotheses. Lecture 1 examines why Draper
regards the biologically patterned distribution of pain and pleasure as more
expected on the Hypothesis of Indifference than on theism. Lecture 2 separates
likelihoods, priors, and posterior judgments; tests theodicies as expansions of
theism; and compares Draper's method with Rowe's without double counting their
overlapping evidence.

The graded work is A-01 Journal 8 and cumulative A-02 Retrieval Quiz 4. A
three-hour ungraded A-05 architecture milestone develops the comparative paper
without supplying final prose. The study guide and discussion are ungraded
practice.
