# Instructor Guide — A-05-P03 Probabilistic Comparison Architecture

This guide supports an ungraded process milestone. It must not become a model
paper, completed architecture, source-selection service, or substitute for the
student's judgment.

## Completion audit

Mark complete only when the record includes:

- the two preserved Week 8 questions with accurate status and source control;
- all six Draper source-control rows using Peterson chapter 31;
- a map containing theism, HI, O, the conditional O1/O2/O3 comparisons,
  likelihood conclusion, local evidential result, and prima facie
  significance;
- all probability roles with no reversed conditional or invented number;
- one response audit with independent support, point of contact, predictive
  success, predictive liability, Draper's reply, and conclusion limit;
- a Rowe/Draper comparison with evidential overlap identified;
- one provisional argument and two materially different response selections,
  with rejected alternatives preserved;
- a heading-level outline rather than drafted paper paragraphs;
- a Week 10 action, a time record totaling no more than three hours, and an
  honest AI-use declaration.

Do not assign points or predict the parent paper's grade from completion.

## Source and argument audit

### Draper source control

The required source is Peterson chapter 31, pp. 553–575. The chapter title is
“The Distribution of Pain and Pleasure as Evidence for Atheism.” The original
article has a different verified title and pagination. Require the student to
cite the edition actually used.

The map must preserve:

1. HI's exact focus on nonhuman benevolent or malevolent action;
2. O's inclusion of pain, pleasure, humans, animals, observation, and
   testimony;
3. O1 as moral-agent biologically useful experience;
4. O2 as nonmoral-sentient biologically useful experience, conditioned on O1;
5. O3 as experience not known to be biologically useful, conditioned on O1 and
   O2;
6. P(O given HI) being much greater than P(O given theism); and
7. the distinction between local favoring and Draper's further prima facie
   rejection claim.

Return a map that substitutes naturalism for HI, moral gratuity for biological
gratuity, or posterior probability for likelihood.

## Probability audit

Ask diagnostic questions without filling the student's table:

- “Which side of the conditional contains the evidence?”
- “Did O enter the prior and then enter again as new evidence?”
- “What gives this expansion probability given theism?”
- “Which observation becomes more expected?”
- “Are these two evidence items independent, conditional, or overlapping?”
- “Does your conclusion concern local favoring, posterior preference, or total
  evidence?”

No number is needed. If the student invents a percentage without a model,
require a reasoned ordinal judgment instead.

## Response and comparison audit

Two author names do not establish material difference. Accept pairings such as:

- a theistic expansion that raises P(O given theism) and a cognitive-limit
  undercutter;
- a likelihood objection and independent counterevidence;
- a Rowean direct-theodicy response and a Draperian comparison-class response.

If both responses merely say that God might have a reason, return the record.
The student must identify whether overlapping animal or apparent-gratuity
evidence is being reused and what new inferential work, if any, each argument
performs.

## Scope and sequence

The cumulative A-05 record must show exactly 7.00 hours through Week 9. The
remaining 3.00 hours belong to Week 10. The student may record a skeptical-
theism question but may not claim results from the unassigned Week 10 source.

The outline may include a thesis placeholder and argument functions. It must
not contain polished paragraphs. Do not help the student choose the final
argument, responses, verdict, counterargument, or wording.

## AI, authorship, and safety boundary

AI may clarify instructions or critique a genuine student-created record
against the completion list. It may not create or repair maps, fill tables,
select sources or responses, calculate or assign evidential weight, formulate
the counterargument or reply, or draft parent-paper prose. Require correction
of an incomplete declaration.

No process record should collect graphic examples, personal disclosure, or
case-specific divine explanations. Philosophical uncertainty never suspends
ordinary protection, reporting, treatment, or care.
