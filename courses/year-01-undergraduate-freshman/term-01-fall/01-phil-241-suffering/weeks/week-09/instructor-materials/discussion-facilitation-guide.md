# Instructor Guide — Week 9 Guided Discussion

This guide supports a 24-minute ungraded exercise. It does not supply a model
journal answer, A-05 architecture, or quiz response.

## Facilitation goals

Listen for five controls:

1. HI is stated exactly rather than equated with naturalism.
2. The Draper advocate makes a likelihood claim rather than a posterior or
   certainty claim.
3. O1, O2, and O3 remain conditionally ordered.
4. The theistic respondent names the response's exact evidential role and one
   unmet burden.
5. The auditor distinguishes local evidence, total evidence, and unchanged
   human duties.

## Round-specific checks

### Draper advocate

A successful opening connects useful warning systems to O1, their extension to
nonmoral subjects to O2, and malfunction or token spillover to O3. The student
need not endorse Draper but must explain why biological pattern is claimed to
fit HI better.

Prompt if needed:

- “Which component are you using?”
- “Are you claiming favoring, posterior superiority, or certainty?”
- “Why does pleasure belong in the observation report?”

### Theistic respondent

The response should perform one clear task. Stable order or embodied goods may
raise P(O given theism); cognitive limitation may undercut the likelihood
comparison; independent evidence may offset it later. Bare possibility is not
enough.

Prompt if needed:

- “What changes in the probability map?”
- “What independent reason supports that expansion?”
- “Which part of O remains surprising?”

### Draper reply

Accept challenges involving alternative means, nonmoral-agent reach,
pathological or biologically appropriate excess, weak independent support, or
comparison-class mismatch. Reject motive attacks and the claim that a
theological response is defective simply because it is theological.

### Auditor

The student should identify evidence, hypotheses, controlled background,
likelihood, expansion, dependence, and conclusion ceiling. One side need not
be declared the winner if the uncertainty is argued.

## Common corrections

- “HI proves atheism” becomes “HI entails the denial of Draper's theism, while
  remaining consistent with some supernatural hypotheses.”
- “Useful pain answers Draper” becomes “usefulness is part of the distribution
  Draper compares.”
- “God may have a reason, so O has no force” becomes “a response must show why
  theism or an independently supported expansion makes O more expected.”
- “Rowe plus Draper gives two independent animal arguments” becomes “their
  evidential overlap must be audited before combining force.”

## Safety boundary

Stop the exercise if it elicits a real crisis or disclosure. Do not facilitate
debate over a participant's suffering. Follow appropriate emergency,
safeguarding, legal, clinical, or pastoral procedures. No philosophical verdict
authorizes delayed protection or care.
