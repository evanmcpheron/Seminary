# Instructor Grading Guide — A-01-J08

Apply the public rubric in [../rubric.md](../rubric.md). Do not reward agreement
with Draper or with the curriculum's confession. Reward source accuracy,
argument role, charity, scope, and honest authorship.

## Criterion anchors

### Hypotheses, evidence, and source control, 2 points

Full credit requires recognizable versions of Draper's theism, HI, and O; a
correct scope distinction; and a locatable Peterson chapter 31 page or heading.
Deduct for equating HI with naturalism, atheism in every sense, or malevolence.
An invented location is an integrity issue as well as a content defect.

### Distributional reasoning, 2 points

The selected component must name its subjects and kind of experience, preserve
the direction of Draper's comparison, and give the relevant biological or
moral reason. For O2 and O3, the conditional relation should remain visible.
Biological gratuity must not be treated as moral gratuity.

### Probability-role precision, 2 points

Full credit distinguishes P(O given H) from P(H given O), identifies priors as
pre-update support, and limits the local result. No equation is required.
Unsupported decimals, certainty, or direct inference to naturalism lose credit.

### Charitable response, 2 points

The response should reach a named point: likelihood, comparison class,
expansion, cognitive access, or counterevidence. It must state one burden left
unmet. Bare confession, bare possibility, or a motive explanation does not
count as a serious response.

### Judgment, scope, charity, and integrity, 2 points

Full credit gives a proportionate verdict, declines a token-purpose claim,
recognizes rational disagreement, stays within the word budget, and includes
accurate source and AI documentation. A sentence that blames a sufferer,
discourages care, or invents God's case-specific purpose is deficient.

## Score and feedback

Use the course scale: 9.3–10.0 A range, 8.3–9.2 B range, 7.3–8.2 C range,
6.0–7.2 D range, and below 6.0 F range. Give feedback by naming the exact
defect, such as reversed conditional, broadened HI, collapsed probability
role, weak point of contact, or unsupported scope. Do not write replacement
sentences for the student.

## Integrity and retake control

Handle fabricated sources or concealed prohibited AI authorship under course
policy. If remediation is assigned, retain the original score and record the
remediation and any permitted retake. The facilitator may ask the student to
locate or orally explain a claim but may not reconstruct the journal response.
