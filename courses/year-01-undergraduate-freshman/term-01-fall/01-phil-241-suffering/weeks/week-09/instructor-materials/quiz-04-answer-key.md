# Instructor Answer Key — A-02-Q04

Keep this file separate from the student quiz. Apply the public scoring
standard in [../rubric.md](../rubric.md) and accept philosophically equivalent
wording.

## Part I — 8 points

1. **C** — HI denies benevolent or malevolent nonhuman action as the source of
   earthly sentient nature or condition.
2. **B** — O includes observed and testimonial evidence about pain and pleasure
   among humans and animals.
3. **C** — Antecedent means independent of O's observations and testimony.
4. **D** — Biological nonutility leaves moral or theological justification as
   a separate question.
5. **A** — The likelihood inequality gives O a direction of evidential support;
   it does not settle the posterior or total case.
6. **B** — An expansion needs both sufficient probability given theism and
   predictive improvement concerning O.
7. **B** — Rowe's appearance-to-gratuity bridge and Draper's comparative
   likelihood bridge are distinct.
8. **C** — Independent historical evidence would attempt to offset the local
   force rather than directly change P(O given each hypothesis).

## Part II — 12 points

### Item 9 — 6 points

- **Why usefulness does not answer Draper, 2 points:** biological usefulness is
  part of the pattern to be explained. Draper argues that its systematic link
  to sentient experience is more expected on HI; merely identifying function
  does not show that a good omnipotent creator would choose the system.
- **O1 plus O2 or O3, 2 points:** O1 concerns useful pain and pleasure among
  moral agents. O2 extends the pattern to nonmoral sentient subjects; O3 adds
  pain and pleasure not known to be useful, much of it pathological or
  biologically appropriate spillover.
- **Required improvement, 2 points:** accept a specific proposal showing that
  an independently supported theistic expansion makes the selected pattern
  more expected, addresses alternatives, reaches nonmoral subjects, or explains
  pathological and distributional liabilities.

An answer saying only that pain warns receives no more than 1 point in the
first category because it repeats the evidence without addressing the
comparison.

### Item 10 — 6 points

Award 0.75 point for each of four distinct errors:

- likelihood is confused with posterior probability;
- evidential favoring is confused with certainty;
- HI is confused with naturalism;
- a pairwise comparison is treated as exhaustive;
- priors are declared irrelevant;
- independent counterevidence or total evidence is ignored;
- Rowe's bridge is conflated with Draper's;
- overlapping animal evidence is assumed independent.

Award 1 point for correcting the local conclusion: O favors HI over theism if
the stated likelihood inequality is correct. Award 1 point for locating priors
and counterevidence in the posterior or total-evidence assessment. Award 1
point for explaining that Rowe and Draper may organize overlapping observations
differently and therefore cannot have their force multiplied without a
dependence audit.
