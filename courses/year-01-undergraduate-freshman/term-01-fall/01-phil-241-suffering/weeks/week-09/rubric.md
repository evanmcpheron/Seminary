## A-02-Q04 — Retrieval Quiz 4, 20 points

- Part I: eight multiple-choice items at 1 point each, 8 points total.
- Part II: two short analysis items at 6 points each, 12 points total.
- Item 9: explains why biological usefulness does not answer Draper, 2 points;
  accurately uses O1 and O2 or O3, 2 points; identifies a specific predictive
  improvement required of an expansion, 2 points.
- Item 10: four distinct errors, 0.75 point each; corrected local likelihood
  conclusion, 1 point; priors or counterevidence placement, 1 point;
  dependence or double-counting explanation, 1 point.
- Philosophically equivalent wording receives full credit. The separate
  instructor key controls scoring.
- Missing or false integrity documentation is handled under course policy,
  not silently converted into an ordinary content deduction.

Score interpretation follows the course scale: 18.6–20.0 A range, 16.6–18.5 B
range, 14.6–16.5 C range, 12.0–14.5 D range, and below 12.0 F range.

## A-05-P03 — Probabilistic Comparison Architecture completion standard

This milestone is ungraded and carries no separate points. It is complete when
the record contains:

- the preserved Week 8 questions with honest Week 9 status and exact source
  locations;
- a six-row Draper source-control sheet using Peterson chapter 31;
- a source-faithful Draper map that separates local likelihood and prima facie
  significance;
- a six-row probability audit addressing likelihood, prior, posterior, total
  evidence, expansion, dependence, and conclusion scope;
- one response audit with point of contact, independent support, predictive
  success and liability, Draper's strongest reply, and a limited verdict;
- a Rowe/Draper comparison that identifies overlap and avoids double counting;
- a provisional selection of one argument and two materially different
  responses with reasons;
- a heading-level outline containing comparison, counterargument, limited
  reply, and conclusion boundaries without drafted prose;
- a scheduled Week 10 action, a three-hour time record, and an honest AI-use
  declaration.

An incomplete element is returned for completion. The facilitator may identify
a missing hypothesis, reversed conditional, unsupported prior, duplicated
evidence, weak response difference, or overbroad conclusion. The facilitator
may not select the argument or responses, fill tables, decide evidential
weight, or author paper prose.

## Integrity and safety controls

Do not award content credit for invented quotations, probability values,
source locations, or concealed prohibited AI authorship. No task requires
graphic examples, personal disclosure, an explanation of a real person's
suffering, or withdrawal of ordinary care.
