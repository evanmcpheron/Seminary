# Week 9 Study Guide

This is ungraded guided practice. Budget 36 minutes after completing the
readings and lectures. Work from memory first, then correct in a different
color or with tracked changes using the assigned sources. Do not consult the
graded quiz or instructor materials.

1. Hypothesis of Indifference
2. observation report O
3. antecedent epistemic probability
4. biological usefulness
5. biologically gratuitous
6. likelihood
7. prior probability
8. posterior probability

equivalent. For items 6–8, keep the order of the conditional probability
visible.

## Part 3 — Probability roles, 7 minutes

For each statement, label it likelihood, prior, posterior, or total-evidence
judgment. Then state what additional information would be needed to move to
the next stronger claim.

1. O is more expected on HI than on theism.
2. Before O is considered, theism has stronger support than HI.
3. After O is considered, HI is better supported than theism.
4. After O and all independent theistic evidence are considered, theism
   remains more reasonable.

Explain in one sentence why no percentage follows from these labels alone.
