# Instructor Key — Week 8 Retrieval Practice

This is a correction key for ungraded practice, not an A-02 answer key and not
a bank of questions for later assessment.

1. **B** — The logical problem alleges incompatibility; the evidential problem
   asks how actual evil bears on the reasonableness or probability of theism.
2. **C** — The factual premise asserts that at least some intense suffering
   meets Rowe's greater-good and worse-evil conditions.
3. **A** — The statement reports what is apparent after inquiry without yet
   asserting that no reason exists.
4. **A** — Evidential support is defeasible and does not require certainty.
   Unknown goods remain possible, but their possibility does not by itself
   give the observed evil zero weight.
5. **C** — The response weakens the link from failure to detect a reason to
   probable absence; it does not prove that a reason exists.
6. **B** — The Moore shift reverses the first argument's order using warranted
   theism and the shared moral premise. Its force depends on the grounds for
   theism, not a formal invalidity in Rowe.
7. **Anchor:** prima facie evidence counts against a proposition when isolated;
   a total-evidence verdict weighs all relevant supporting and opposing
   evidence before judging what is reasonable overall.
8. **Anchor:** the possible reason would belong to an omniscient sovereign's
   complete field of action, while a human agent lacks that knowledge and
   remains governed by ordinary duties to protect, report, relieve harm, and
   seek appropriate care.

Accept equivalent concise answers for items 7–8. Require correction if item 8
uses providence to excuse passivity or assigns a case-specific divine purpose.
