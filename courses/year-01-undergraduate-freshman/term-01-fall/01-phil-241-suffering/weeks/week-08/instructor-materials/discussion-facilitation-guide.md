# Instructor Guide — Week 8 Evidential-Inference Discussion

## Purpose and conditions

Keep the exercise to 30 minutes and the source-supplied hypothetical case. Do
not invite personal trauma, graphic detail, or research into an actual tragedy.
The goal is to expose the inductive bridge, not to make participants defend
their faith identity or disclose grief.

## Strong-role anchors

### Rowean analyst

A strong presentation should say that:

- the argument concedes possible compatibility;
- no qualifying good is apparent for the focal case after reflection;
- human beings have some grasp of significant goods and moral relations;
- variety and profusion strengthen the case that at least one evil is
  gratuitous; and
- the conclusion is evidential or probabilistic, not certain.

### Inference critic

A strong presentation should grant the badness and apparent pointlessness of
the suffering, then ask whether:

- a divine reason would be expected to be detectable by finite observers;
- human goods are representative of all goods and relations;
- the search domain includes remote, counterfactual, communal, and
  eschatological relations; and
- lowering confidence in the bridge warrants suspension rather than the
  opposite factual conclusion.

### Scope and ethics keeper

The keeper should identify:

- undercutting versus rebutting;
- isolated evil evidence versus total evidence;
- standard theism versus a revised divine concept;
- possible, probable, and established conclusions; and
- divine permission versus a human duty to intervene.

## Useful interventions

- “Which exact premise or bridge does that point address?”
- “Would this show that a reason exists or only that its absence is not
  established?”
- “What would make the reason detectable if it existed?”
- “Does the reply fit this case or only a class of cases?”
- “Are you evaluating evil alone or total evidence?”
- “Which stronger conclusion have you declined?”

Redirect “the atheist has no faith” and “the Christian ignores evidence” as
motive attacks. Redirect “God must have done it for growth” as an unsupported
token explanation. Redirect “mystery means do nothing” to the moral-asymmetry
boundary and immediate human duties.

## Completion check

The final four sentences should name evidence, bridge, defeater, and scope.
The duty sentence should call for protection, reporting, relief, treatment,
truth-telling, or practical care as appropriate. Do not score the exercise or
infer a participant's spiritual condition from the role performed.
