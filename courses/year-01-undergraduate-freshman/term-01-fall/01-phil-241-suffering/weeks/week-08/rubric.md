# Week 8 Evaluation Standards

| Criterion | Excellent | Proficient | Developing | Deficient | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Source accuracy and location | States Rowe's claim accurately, preserves both gratuity conditions, distinguishes Rowe from Trakakis, and supplies a precise assigned location. | Substantially accurate with one minor omission or location weakness. | General idea is recognizable, but a condition, voice, or location is blurred. | Misattributes the claim, invents a location, or gives no usable source control. | 2 |
| Argument structure and vocabulary | Correctly separates appearance, factual premise, theological premise, conclusion, deductive validity, and inductive support. | Most levels are correct with one minor conflation. | Two important levels or inference types are blurred. | Treats appearance as proof, misstates the compact argument, or supplies no reconstruction. | 2 |
| Inductive-bridge analysis | Names the detectability issue, gives a reason the evidence has force, and states a genuine limit without false precision. | Bridge and force/limit are substantially clear with one gap. | Mentions ignorance but does not analyze how it affects the inference. | Ignores the bridge or asserts certainty without support. | 2 |
| Charitable response | Presents a strong response, identifies its point of contact, and correctly classifies its rebutting, undercutting, counterevidential, or target-revising role. | Relevant and fair response with one role or scope imprecision. | Response is generic, weakly connected, or partly caricatured. | Straw man, motive attack, or no material response. | 2 |
| Judgment, charity, clarity, and integrity | Gives a warranted conclusion ceiling, names a stronger unwarranted claim, recognizes rational disagreement, writes clearly within the limit, cites honestly, and declares AI use. | Sound limited judgment with one minor clarity or documentation issue. | Judgment is underexplained, overbroad, or missing one required integrity element. | Confessional agreement substitutes for analysis, opposing belief is ridiculed, a hidden purpose is assigned, or citation/authorship is false. | 2 |
|  |  |  |  | **Total** | **10** |

The twelve A-01 entries are averaged and together constitute 10 percent of the
course grade. Agreement with Rowe or with the course's confession is not itself
a scoring criterion. Accuracy, argument analysis, charity, and scope control
are.

## A-05-P02 — Evidential Argument Dossier completion standard

This milestone is ungraded and carries no separate points. It is complete when
the dossier contains:

- the preserved Week 7 architecture and a `retain`/`revise`/`defer` change log;
- exact, non-invented assigned locations for Rowe and Trakakis with source
  wording distinguished from student reconstruction;
- both compact and expanded maps, with added bridges labeled and conclusion
  scope stated;
- a five-row inference audit addressing detectability, competence, domain,
  sample, and conclusion ceiling;
- two materially different response profiles using verified, assigned, and
  actually read sources;
- a strong counterargument with a named point of contact and a limited reply;
- three relevant comparison criteria and two questions reserved for Weeks
  9–10;
- a scheduled next action and accurate cumulative time record; and
- an honest AI-use declaration.

An incomplete element is returned for completion. The facilitator may identify
a missing premise, unsupported attribution, duplicated response, untested
bridge, or scope problem. The facilitator may not choose the parent paper's
argument, responses, thesis, judgments, or wording for the student.

## Integrity and safety controls

Do not award content credit for invented quotations, page ranges, sources, or
claims. Apply course policy to concealed prohibited AI authorship rather than
silently repairing it. No assignment requires graphic case details, personal
trauma disclosure, a token explanation, or outside tragedy research.
