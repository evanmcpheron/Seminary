# Lecture 2 Outline — From Apparently Gratuitous Suffering to Evidential Judgment

## Objectives

- Distinguish the appearance claim, factual claim, and atheistic conclusion.
- Expose the inductive bridge from an undisclosed reason to a probably absent
  reason.
- Evaluate when failure to find something is evidence of its absence.
- Compare rebutting and undercutting responses to Rowe's inference.
- Separate local evidence from a total-evidence verdict.
- Preserve ordinary moral action under uncertainty about providence.
- Build a disciplined conclusion-scale for A-05 analysis.
