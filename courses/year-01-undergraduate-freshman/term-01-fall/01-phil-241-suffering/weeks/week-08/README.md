# Week 8 — The Evidential Problem of Evil

## Weekly purpose

Week 8 begins the course's second movement by asking what evil counts as
evidence even after strict logical inconsistency has been set aside. Lecture 1
reconstructs William L. Rowe's case from intense, apparently gratuitous
suffering and distinguishes its deductive core from the inductive support for
its factual premise. Lecture 2 analyzes the move from “no justifying reason is
apparent to us” to “probably no justifying reason exists,” compares major reply
strategies, and calibrates what a conclusion may responsibly claim.

The graded work is A-01 Journal 7. A three-hour, ungraded A-05 dossier develops
the later comparative paper without supplying its thesis or prose. The study
guide, discussion, and retrieval check are ungraded practice; no A-02 quiz is
scheduled this week.
