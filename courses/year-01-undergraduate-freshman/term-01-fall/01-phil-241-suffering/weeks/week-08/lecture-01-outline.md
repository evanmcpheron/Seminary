# Lecture 1 Outline — Rowe's Evidential Argument from Suffering

## Objectives

- Distinguish Rowe's evidential project from Mackie's logical challenge.
- Reconstruct Rowe's 1979 argument without turning its factual premise into an
  observation report.
- Explain the greater-good and worse-evil conditions in the moral premise.
- Analyze the fawn case as inductive support rather than proof.
- Compare direct attack and G. E. Moore shift responses.
- Relate Rowe's friendly atheism to charitable academic engagement.
- State a confessionally Christian response without claiming a hidden purpose
  for a particular instance of suffering.
