# Week 4 Evaluation Standards

## A-01-J04 — Reading and Argument Journal 4, 10 points

| Criterion | Outcome | Excellent | Proficient | Developing | Deficient | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Source claim and location | LO-01, LO-05 | States a precise, locatable Mackie or Beebe claim without conflating source voices. | Claim is substantially accurate and locatable with one minor imprecision. | Claim is broad, loosely located, or partly conflates sources. | Misrepresents the source or provides no usable location. | 2 |
| Logical role | LO-01 | Correctly identifies the claim's role and explains its work in the argument. | Role is correct but explanation is brief. | Label or explanation is partly mistaken. | Merely names a term or assigns the wrong function. | 2 |
| Strong pressure | LO-02, LO-03, LO-05 | Presses a serious premise, inference, or limitation after representing the target charitably. | Relevant and fair with minor underdevelopment. | Objection is vague or only partly reaches the target. | Caricatures, attacks motives, or supplies no genuine pressure. | 2 |
| Scope control | LO-01, LO-02 | States both the warranted conclusion and a specific unwarranted larger conclusion. | Scope is mostly accurate with one minor overstatement. | Leaves the boundary unclear or conflates possibility with actuality/probability. | Advertises a result the argument cannot support. | 2 |
| Judgment, clarity, and declaration | LO-03 | Gives a balanced provisional judgment; prose, source entry, metadata, and AI declaration are complete. | Substantially complete with a minor defect. | Judgment is one-sided or documentation defects impede review. | No reasoned judgment, fabricated source, or missing declaration. | 2 |
|  |  |  |  |  | **Total** | **10** |

Score interpretation follows the course scale: 9.3–10.0 A range, 8.3–9.2 B
range, 7.3–8.2 C range, 6.0–7.2 D range, and below 6.0 F range.

## A-02-Q02 — Retrieval Quiz 2, 20 points

- Part I: eight multiple-choice items at 1 point each, 8 points total.
- Part II: two short argument-analysis items at 6 points each, 12 points total.
- For each Part II answer: accurate distinction or diagnosis, 2 points;
  correct argument application, 2 points; scope-controlled explanation, 2
  points.
- Minor wording differences receive full credit when the philosophical content
  is equivalent. The separate instructor key controls scoring.
- Missing or false integrity documentation is handled under the course policy,
  not silently converted into an ordinary content deduction.

Score interpretation follows the course scale: 18.6–20.0 A range, 16.6–18.5 B
range, 14.6–16.5 C range, 12.0–14.5 D range, and below 12.0 F range.

## A-03 — Argument Reconstruction Brief, 100 points

| Criterion | Outcomes | Excellent | Proficient | Developing | Deficient | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Source identity, attribution, and target | LO-01 | Precisely identifies Philo, Cleanthes's empirical inference, the assigned location, and all sources actually used. | Substantially accurate with a minor attribution or location defect. | Target or speaker is vague, or a source is difficult to locate. | Misattributes the argument, invents a source, or does not establish a primary-text basis. | 15 |
| Premise-conclusion reconstruction | LO-01, LO-02 | A clear 5–7-premise map captures the source's reasoning; added premises are labeled; the conclusion follows or is supported as claimed. | Map is accurate and coherent with a minor gap or granularity problem. | Map omits an important step, blurs source and reconstruction, or weakens the inference. | Map is not recognizable, is substantially invalid as presented, or is externally supplied. | 25 |
| Logical classification and analysis | LO-01, LO-02 | Correctly distinguishes evidential support from strict contradiction and analyzes validity/support using controlled terms. | Classification is correct and analysis substantially sound with minor imprecision. | Terms are used inconsistently or analysis is mostly asserted. | Treats Philo's argument as a bare contradiction or cannot assess its logical form. | 15 |
| Ambiguity or suppressed premise | LO-01, LO-02 | Identifies one consequential issue and explains exactly how it affects the inference. | Identifies a relevant issue and gives a substantially correct effect. | Names an issue without tracing its role. | Omits the requirement or supplies an unrelated observation. | 10 |
| Strong objection | LO-02 | Presents a charitable, source-relevant objection that reaches a premise or inference. | Fair and relevant but not fully developed. | Objection is weak, vague, or partly misdirected. | Caricatures the position, attacks motives, or gives no genuine objection. | 10 |
| Theistic reply and point of contact | LO-01, LO-02 | Accurately states a reply, identifies its exact target, and judges whether it answers all or part of the argument. | Reply and point of contact are substantially accurate with a minor omission. | Reply is recognizable but its target or scope is unclear. | Misrepresents the reply or treats bare compatibility as complete evidential success. | 10 |
| Preliminary evaluation and scope | LO-02 | Gives a reasoned, balanced judgment and distinguishes warranted from unwarranted conclusions. | Judgment is supported and mostly scope-controlled. | Judgment is thin, one-sided, or partly overclaims. | Merely announces agreement/disagreement or makes a major scope error. | 10 |
| Documentation, revision evidence, clarity, and AI declaration | LO-01 | Metadata, notes, bibliography, declaration, word range, and three-item change log are complete; prose is clear; D01/D02 are preserved. | One minor documentation, clarity, or process defect. | Multiple defects impede review, or revision evidence is thin. | Fabricated citation, missing declaration, absent process evidence, or prose not assessable as the student's own. | 5 |
|  |  |  |  |  | **Total** | **100** |

The undergraduate proficiency threshold is 73 points. Confessional agreement
does not earn points, and a skeptical conclusion is not penalized when the
source, reasoning, and scope standards are met.

## Integrity control

Fabricated citations, concealed prohibited AI authorship, quiz assistance, or
other integrity concerns are handled under the course policy rather than
quietly repaired through scoring. Instructor feedback may identify a missing
step or criterion but may not write the student's reconstruction, objection,
reply, or evaluative prose.
