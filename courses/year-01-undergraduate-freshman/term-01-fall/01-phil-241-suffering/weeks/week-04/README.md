# Week 4 — The Logical Problem of Evil

## Weekly purpose

Week 4 reconstructs the strict claim that classical theism and evil are
logically inconsistent. Lecture 1 distinguishes direct contradiction from an
indirectly inconsistent set and makes Mackie's bridge principles explicit.
Lecture 2 presents Mackie's criticisms of common theistic replies before
examining the limited consistency-model response described by Beebe.

The week trains the student to state both conclusions modestly: the initial
triad needs substantive connecting premises, and a possible morally sufficient
reason can answer a universal impossibility claim without explaining every
actual evil. The distinction prepares Week 5's primary-source study of the
free-will defense and Weeks 8–10 on evidential arguments.
