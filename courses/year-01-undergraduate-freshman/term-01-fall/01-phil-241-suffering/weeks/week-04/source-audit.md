## Mackie argument audit

- The lectures do not misidentify the initial triad as a direct contradiction.
  They make explicit the connecting premises Mackie himself says are needed.
- The formal reconstruction adds omniscience as part of the classical-theist
  target and labels it as an explicit premise in a fuller reconstruction; it
  does not pretend that Mackie's opening three-item statement contains that
  separate line.
- Mackie's principle that goodness opposes and eliminates evil as far as it can
  and his unrestricted power principle are paraphrased rather than quoted.
- The discussion of adequate solutions accurately reports that rejecting or
  qualifying a member of the inconsistent set removes the contradiction while
  distinguishing logical adequacy from theological acceptability.
- The four response families—counterpart, means-end, higher-order-good, and
  free-will—are presented as arguments with identifiable pressure points. No
  defender is caricatured as simply approving evil.
- The paradox-of-omnipotence discussion is presented as pressure on an
  unrestricted concept of power, not as a stand-alone proof that every concept
  of omnipotence is incoherent.

## Beebe and theistic-reply audit

- Beebe's official entry confirms that consistency means possible joint truth,
  not actual truth or probability, and that one possible countermodel is enough
  to rebut a universal impossibility claim.
- The lectures accurately use *morally sufficient reason* for a reason that
  would justify permission, while refusing to infer an actual divine reason
  from bare possibility.
- The free-will model distinguishes a world in which agents contingently choose
  well from a world in which God causally determines choices while those same
  choices remain undetermined in the relevant sense.
- The report that the defense is widely taken to rebut the classical logical
  problem is attributed to Beebe's survey. The lectures immediately preserve
  the remaining explanatory and evidential questions.
- Natural evil, heavenly freedom, divine freedom, and actual-world distribution
  are stated as continuing objections. Week 5's assigned Plantinga study is not
  preempted or presented as already complete.

## Formal-logic and claim-label audit

- The lectures distinguish proposition, conjunction, contradiction,
  consistency, entailment, validity, soundness, possibility, and probability.
- Added premises are identified as bridge principles or lecture
  reconstructions, not quotations from Mackie or Beebe.
- The unqualified and morally qualified prevention principles are separated so
  the shift from logical to evidential burden remains visible.
- Primary-source reports, broad scholarly consensus, contested evaluation,
  confessional conclusions, and curriculum synthesis are signaled where
  confusion is likely.
- Defeating the logical inconsistency claim is never described as proving
  theism, identifying God's actual reasons, or resolving the whole problem of
  evil.

## Scripture and theological audit

- The course's classical divine-attribute commitments are exposed to the
  logical test rather than protected from criticism by assertion.
- Genesis 1–3, Job, the lament psalms, James 1, Romans 8, and Revelation 21–22
  are used within the approved canonical framework. They are not represented
  as premises Mackie accepts or as verbatim presentations of modal logic.
- The confessional analysis follows charitable reconstruction and labels
  itself as independent curriculum judgment, not official Wheaton College
  teaching.
- The lectures retain real evil, creaturely responsibility, lament, divine
  providence, judgment, and hope without claiming that any text reveals the
  reason for a particular tragedy.

## Pastoral, student-safety, and copyright audit

- No personal disclosure is required and no real sufferer is used as a case
  study.
- The lectures distinguish a formal reply from bedside care and affirm safety,
  presence, lament, practical aid, justice, and qualified referral.
- The free-will and providence discussions do not excuse perpetrators or human
  failure to prevent harm.
- The lectures consist of original synthesis and concise argument maps. They
  reproduce no extended passage from Mackie, Beebe, Peterson, or a copyrighted
  Bible edition.
