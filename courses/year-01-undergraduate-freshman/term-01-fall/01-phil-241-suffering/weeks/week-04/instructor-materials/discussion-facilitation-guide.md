# Instructor Facilitation Guide — Week 4 Consistency Hearing

## Purpose and preparation

The exercise evaluates oral practice, not confessional assent. Have Mackie's
assigned chapter, Beebe's entry, both audited lectures, and the Week 3
compatibility/evidence notes available. Require source attribution before
evaluation.

## Facilitation sequence

1. Confirm that each role has a claim, reason, objection, and location.
2. During the Mackie hearing, display four columns: **starting claim**,
   **bridge principle**, **derived claim**, and **objection**.
3. During the countermodel hearing, display **possible**, **actual**,
   **probable**, and **pastorally relevant**. Require every conclusion to occupy
   one column.
4. Ask the logical auditor to track every universal quantifier and every shift
   in the meanings of goodness, power, and freedom.
5. Reserve the final 20 minutes for the scope verdict and sentence-level
   revision.

## Source anchors

- Mackie acknowledges that the opening claims need added principles connecting
  goodness, evil, and omnipotence.
- His adequate solutions explicitly reject or qualify a premise; his
  fallacious-solution criticism alleges covert revision while the original
  words are retained.
- Beebe distinguishes possible joint truth from plausibility and actual truth.
- A morally sufficient reason is not merely any motive; it must make permission
  morally consistent with perfect goodness.
- A free-will model must distinguish contingent right choice from divine
  causal determination of a choice described as undetermined in the relevant
  sense.
- Natural evil, heaven, divine freedom, and distribution remain live pressures.

## Corrections to make promptly

- “The triad is visibly contradictory.” Ask which line is the negation of
  another or which bridge derives the negation.
- “A possible story proves God exists.” Ask whether a countermodel establishes
  consistency or truth.
- “Free will explains earthquakes.” Ask which causal step connects the two.
- “Mackie ignores all theistic replies.” Require the advocate to identify the
  reply Mackie actually analyzes and his specific objection.
- “Logical defeat means evil is not serious.” Ask which moral or existential
  proposition follows from consistency alone.

## Pastoral and discussion boundaries

Do not invite a participant to supply personal suffering as evidence. The
fictional two-setting contrast exists to distinguish philosophical relevance
from pastoral timing. If a real disclosure concerns danger, abuse, or risk of
harm, stop the exercise and follow the course safety and referral policy.

## Completion evidence

A completed session includes one source-controlled reconstruction, one
reasoned countermodel ruling, four scope verdicts, a joint four-sentence report,
and one private revision per participant. Do not grade or collect the private
revision unless later delivery procedures require formative documentation.
