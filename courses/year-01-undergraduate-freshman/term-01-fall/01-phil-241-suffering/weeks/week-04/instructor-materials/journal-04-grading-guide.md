# Instructor Grading Guide — A-01-J04

Use the public 10-point rubric in `../rubric.md`. This guide clarifies evidence
and scope standards; it does not supply a model response.

## Threshold checks

Confirm that the response:

- is 300–400 words, excluding metadata and source entry;
- uses all five required headings;
- selects one locatable Mackie or Beebe claim;
- includes a complete source entry and AI-use declaration; and
- does not infer an actual case-specific divine reason from possibility.

Minor word-count or documentation defects ordinarily affect the final
criterion. Fabricated evidence or concealed prohibited AI authorship is an
integrity issue.

## Content guidance

High-quality work should identify the selected claim as an initial
proposition, bridge, definition, premise revision, countermodel, or objection
and explain how it changes the argument. The strong-pressure section must reach
a premise, inference, or scope limit after representing the target accurately.

The scope section should distinguish at least one of these pairs:

- consistency / truth;
- possibility / actuality;
- possibility / probability;
- moral evil / natural evil; or
- formal answer / pastoral response.

Do not deduct for agreement or disagreement with the course's confessional
judgment when the source, reasoning, and scope standards are met.

## Feedback boundary

Identify the criterion and ask a focused question such as “Which premise does
this objection challenge?” or “Does this model establish possibility or
actuality?” Do not choose the source claim, formulate the pressure, or rewrite
the student's prose.
