# Lecture 1 Outline — Defense, Theodicy, and the Explanatory Burden

## Objectives

- Distinguish logical, evidential, explanatory, existential, and pastoral
  questions.
- Compare the aims and burdens of a defense and a theodicy.
- Explain how possible truth can answer a consistency objection without
  becoming an actual explanation.
- Identify Lewis's account as more ambitious than a bare consistency defense.
- Evaluate Lewis's claims about human pain, surrender, compassion, and complex
  good without treating pain or wrongdoing as good in itself.
- Form a restrained confessional synthesis concerning freedom, providence,
  responsibility, and care.
