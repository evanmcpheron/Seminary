# Week 5 — Free Will and Moral Evil

## Weekly purpose

Week 5 develops the free-will response introduced in Week 4. Lecture 1
distinguishes a defense, which answers a specified objection by showing a
possible consistency model, from a theodicy, which proposes an account of
God's actual reasons. It uses Lewis's account of human pain to show both the
additional explanatory claims a theodicy makes and the moral restraints that
must govern their use. Lecture 2 reconstructs Plantinga's technical reply to
Mackie's “always freely choose the good” objection, including possible and
feasible worlds, significant freedom, divine actualization, and transworld
depravity.

The week reaches a deliberately limited conclusion. Plantinga offers a serious
answer to the strict logical problem concerning moral evil. His defense does
not by itself establish libertarian freedom, identify God's actual reason,
explain natural evil, justify this world's amount and distribution of harm, or
supply an immediate pastoral response.
