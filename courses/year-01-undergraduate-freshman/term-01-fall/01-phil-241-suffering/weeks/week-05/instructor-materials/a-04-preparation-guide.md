# Instructor Guide — A-04-P01 Midterm Preparation 1

This guide supports a required ungraded preparation milestone. It is not an
exam, practice exam, answer key, or disclosure of later midterm questions.

## Completion audit

Mark the milestone complete only when the record contains:

- a Weeks 1–5 coverage grid naming topics, source voices, and linked outcomes;
- twelve definitions visibly attempted from memory and then corrected;
- accurate entries for *defense*, *theodicy*, *feasible world*, and
  *transworld depravity*;
- two numbered, source-attributed argument maps first attempted from memory;
- a map that distinguishes Mackie's always-right-world objection from
  Plantinga's feasibility response;
- one serious objection aimed at a premise or inference in each map;
- at least three visible scope or attribution corrections;
- a specific three-item remediation plan with dates or study blocks; and
- an honest record of permitted assistance.

Do not attach points or fold completion into the A-04 exam score.

## Diagnostic categories

Record only the category and a prompt for student repair:

- **Definition:** a term is circular, conflated, or missing its limiting
  condition.
- **Attribution:** an author, speaker, or source location is confused.
- **Structure:** a premise, bridge, inference, or conclusion is missing.
- **Scope:** possibility becomes actuality, consistency becomes truth, or a
  moral-evil argument is extended to natural evil without support.
- **Objection fit:** the objection does not touch a premise or inference.
- **Retrieval process:** sources or assistance were used during the closed-book
  phase.
- **Remediation:** the next action is vague or unscheduled.

Useful prompts include “Which premise carries that inference?”, “Is this world
possible or feasible?”, and “What source voice supports this line?” Do not
supply a corrected map, replacement objection, model response, or predicted
exam answer.

## Remediation routing

- Send definition errors to the relevant completed week's study guide and
  lecture glossary.
- Send attribution errors to the assigned reading and source-location record.
- Send structural errors to the argument-map exercises already completed in
  Weeks 1–5.
- Send Week 5 scope errors to the defense/theodicy and possible/feasible
  exercises in `../study-guide.md`.
- Require the student to revise the remediation plan if it lacks a named task,
  duration, and study date or block.

## Integrity boundary

The closed-book definitions and initial maps must remain the student's own
retrieval. Assistance may clarify a named concept only after the correction
record is complete and may not produce or repair maps, objections, remediation
plans, or future exam responses. Preserve the preparation record as evidence
of process without turning this milestone into a graded assessment.
