# Week 5 Retrieval Practice — Free Will and Moral Evil

This is **ungraded practice**, not an A-02 quiz. Work without sources for 20
minutes. Then use the assigned readings and lectures for 10 minutes to correct
the work in a different color or under a **Correction** label. Do not consult
the instructor key until both phases are complete.

## Part I — Retrieve the distinctions

Answer in one or two sentences each.

1. Distinguish a defense from a theodicy.
2. Define *morally significant freedom* in Plantinga's model.
3. Distinguish a possible world from a feasible world.
4. Why can God not causally determine a choice and leave it
   libertarian-free in the same respect?
5. What is a counterfactual of creaturely freedom?
6. State transworld depravity without saying that all free creatures
   necessarily sin.
7. Does Plantinga deny that an always-right free world is possible?
8. Distinguish Lewis's simple evil from complex good.

## Part II — Diagnose the scope

For each statement, identify one accurate element and correct one error.

9. “Plantinga proves that every actual person has transworld depravity.”
10. “A possible world of people who always freely do right must be a world God
    can actualize while preserving their freedom.”
11. “Because freedom may explain moral evil, it also explains earthquakes and
    animal suffering.”
12. “If God brings good from a wrongful act, the wrongdoer was serving the good
    and should not be blamed.”
