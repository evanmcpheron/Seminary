# Week 5 Evaluation Standards

## A-01-J05 — Reading and Argument Journal 5, 10 points

| Criterion | Outcome | Excellent | Proficient | Developing | Deficient | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Source claim and location | LO-01 | States a precise, locatable Plantinga or Lewis claim without conflating source voices. | Claim is substantially accurate and locatable with one minor imprecision. | Claim is too broad, loosely located, or partly conflates sources. | Misrepresents the source or supplies no usable location. | 2 |
| Argumentative status | LO-01, LO-03 | Correctly classifies defense, theodicy, or supporting claim and explains the burden involved. | Classification and explanation are substantially correct with a minor omission. | Label is plausible but the burden or purpose is unclear. | Conflates possibility with actuality or assigns the wrong argumentative task. | 2 |
| Reasons and role | LO-01, LO-03 | Reconstructs at least two source-controlled reasons or distinctions and shows their work in the argument. | Reasons and role are accurate but briefly developed. | Gives incomplete reasons or mostly summarizes. | Supplies little source reasoning or attributes a reconstruction to the author as quotation. | 2 |
| Strong objection | LO-02, LO-03 | Presents a fair, serious objection and identifies its exact target category. | Objection is relevant and fair with minor underdevelopment. | Objection is vague, weak, or partly misclassified. | Caricatures, attacks motives, or provides no genuine pressure. | 2 |
| Judgment, scope, clarity, and declaration | LO-03, LO-06 | Gives a balanced judgment, states warranted and unwarranted conclusions, supplies a responsible Christian qualification, and completes documentation. | Substantially complete with a minor defect. | Judgment is one-sided, scope is blurred, or documentation defects impede review. | Claims a case-specific divine reason, excuses wrongdoing, fabricates a source, or omits the declaration. | 2 |
|  |  |  |  |  | **Total** | **10** |

Score interpretation follows the course scale: 9.3–10.0 A range, 8.3–9.2 B
range, 7.3–8.2 C range, 6.0–7.2 D range, and below 6.0 F range.

## A-04-P01 — Midterm preparation completion standard

This Week 5 milestone carries no separate points and must not be averaged into
the A-04 midterm score. It is complete when the preparation record contains:

- a Weeks 1–5 grid with topics, source voices, and outcomes;
- twelve definitions first written from memory and then visibly corrected;
- the four required Week 5 terms;
- two numbered and attributed argument maps produced first from memory;
- Mackie's always-right-world objection distinguished from Plantinga's reply;
- one serious objection aimed at a premise or inference in each map;
- at least three documented scope or attribution corrections;
- a specific three-item remediation plan; and
- an honest record of any permitted assistance.

Incomplete elements are returned for completion. The facilitator may identify
a missing distinction or source location but may not write the student's
argument map, objection, or later exam response.

## Ungraded retrieval practice

The Week 5 retrieval practice carries no points and is not an A-02 assessment.
Completion means the student attempted all items without sources, completed the
source-check phase, and recorded at least two corrections. The separate
instructor key supports self-correction only.

## Integrity control

Fabricated citations, concealed prohibited AI authorship, or assistance during
closed-book retrieval are handled under the course policy rather than quietly
repaired through scoring. Evaluation concerns accurate source representation,
argument classification, charitable objection, and scope-controlled judgment,
not assent to the curriculum's confessional conclusion.
