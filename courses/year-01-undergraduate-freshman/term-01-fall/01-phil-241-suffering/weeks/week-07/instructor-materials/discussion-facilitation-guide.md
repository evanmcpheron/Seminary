# Instructor Guide — Week 7 Guided Discussion

## Facilitation aim

Keep the exchange on the relation between eschatological hope and theodical
burden. Do not invite personal disclosure or turn the discussion into a vote
for or against Christianity.

## Strong-role anchors

- The **constructive interpreter** should say that heaven belongs to the
  Christian worldview being evaluated and that Lewis portrays communion with
  God as fulfillment internal to rightly ordered desire, not merely an
  unrelated prize.
- The **critical analyst** should ask whether the future good is compensation,
  outweighing, or defeat; whether the sufferer benefits; whether the route was
  necessary; and whether less harmful alternatives were feasible.
- The **scope keeper** should connect Romans 8's groaning and liberation and
  Revelation 21–22's renewed creation to hope, while refusing the inference
  that either text reveals God's reason for a token loss.

## Useful prompts

- “Which premise connects the promised end to permission of this means?”
- “Who bears the cost and who receives the proposed good?”
- “Would your conclusion survive if the amount or distribution changed?”
- “Is that claim general, kind-level, or token?”
- “What does this passage state, and what are you inferring?”

## Completion audit

The joint verdict should contain three parts: chapter 10 adds a necessary
Christian horizon; it does not prove the necessity or justification of every
evil; and protection, treatment, lament, truth-telling, justice, and care
remain duties. Correct a role that caricatures its opponent before permitting
the final synthesis.
