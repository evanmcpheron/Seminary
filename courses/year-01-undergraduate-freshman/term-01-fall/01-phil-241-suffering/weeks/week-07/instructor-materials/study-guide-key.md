# Instructor Key — Week 7 Study Guide

Keep this file separate until the student has attempted the ungraded guide.
Equivalent precise answers are acceptable. These anchors diagnose scope; they
are not model prose for a graded submission.

## 1. Modal and conclusion-scope sort

1. **Possible.** Coherence supplies a candidate consistency model. Moving to
   feasible needs facts about actualizability; moving to actual or known needs
   evidence about God's reason.
2. **Possible.** A complete world can be coherent while not feasible for God
   to actualize with those same undetermined creaturely choices.
3. **Actual** as the stipulated modest observation; it does not establish that
   this route is **necessary**, comparatively best, or sufficient to justify
   actual amount and distribution.
4. **Actual** as a stipulated result. The result alone does not establish a
   necessary route, divine intention, permission justified all things
   considered, or a general rule.
5. **Actual textual/theological claim** about what Romans 8 asserts within the
   Christian model. It does not make a token causal explanation known.

A defense establishes possible compatibility, but does not establish God's
actual reason, a full theodicy, evidential probability, or token explanation.

## 2. Source-role relay

| Source voice | Principal question | Central contribution | Does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Augustine | What is evil, and how does will become disordered? | Evil as corruption/privation of created good; moral disorder of will | Why every particular evil is permitted |
| Aquinas | How can causes, defect, fault, and penalty be distinguished? | Direct/accidental causation and creaturely defect | Divine absence from providence or a complete natural-evil theodicy |
| Philo | What do the world's experienced features support about divine attributes? | Compatibility differs from evidential inference | A formal contradiction merely from the initial triad |
| Mackie | Are theism and evil strictly inconsistent given strong bridges? | Makes bridge principles and unstable replies explicit | Every later evidential conclusion without further premises |
| Plantinga | Can a possibly true freedom model block inconsistency? | Significant freedom, feasibility, possible transworld depravity | Actual divine reason, probability, or full natural-evil account |
| Swinburne | How might natural processes support knowledge and choice? | Stable consequences and serious moral opportunity | Necessity, better-means, amount, or distribution by itself |
| Stump | Has the proposed natural-evil route been shown necessary or better? | Necessity and alternative-means pressures | That no natural evil could ever be justified |
| Lewis | How might a cumulative Christian worldview interpret pain? | Coherent power, perfecting love, rebellion, possible purposes, judgment, animals, heaven | A verified token reason or complete justification of every evil |

A good solid line might connect Mackie and Plantinga or Swinburne and Stump. A
good dashed line might connect Augustine and Plantinga, since privation and a
consistency defense principally answer different questions.

## 3. Eschatology and value-relation check

1. compensation;
2. outweighing;
3. defeat;
4. eschatological promise;
5. token explanation.

Claim 5 could include a proposed defeat or necessary-means relation, but the
token assertion exceeds the evidence. Chapter 10 places suffering within final
hope and portrays the good; it does not demonstrate the necessity of each
particular route.

## 4. Biblical boundary

Accept answers that identify at least two of these errors:

- creaturely ignorance is not itself an explanation or demonstration of a
  morally sufficient reason;
- Job's reader has information unavailable to Job, while the divine speeches
  do not give Job a token causal account;
- the friends' confident application is rebuked rather than authorized;
- Romans 8 calls creation's state groaning and bondage, not good in itself;
- future glory, conformity, and liberation do not entail that every harmful
  event is a necessary means;
- general providence and hope do not yield a token explanation.

The corrected response should preserve lament, care, protection, and hope.
