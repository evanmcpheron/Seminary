# Instructor Key — Week 7 Midterm Readiness Check

Release only after the student completes the eight-minute closed-book phase.
This practice carries no points and must not be entered as A-02 or A-04.

1. **B.** A defense needs a possibly true model sufficient to answer strict
   inconsistency; it need not identify God's actual reason.
2. **C.** Conceivability or logical possibility does not establish divine
   feasibility when the description also requires the choices to remain the
   creatures' undetermined free acts.
3. **C.** Privation is corruption or lack in a good created nature, not denial
   that the deprivation or injury is real.
4. **B.** The pressure asks whether a less costly feasible route could supply
   the relevant knowledge or good.
5. A sufficient answer distinguishes an evidential question about what the
   world supports or makes likely from a strict question about whether a set
   of claims entails a contradiction.
6. A sufficient answer states that hope identifies a promised end or final
   horizon, while a token explanation asserts why this particular evil was
   permitted and therefore requires event-specific warrant.

If items 5–6 remain vague, route the student to Lecture 2's decision tree and
Lecture 1's section on value relations. Do not supply, predict, or discuss the
actual midterm questions.
