# Instructor Guide — A-05-P01 Comparison Planning

This guide supports an ungraded planning milestone. It must not become a model
paper, source-selection service, or substitute for the student's judgment.

## Completion audit

Mark complete only when the planning record includes:

- a neutral provisional question with a named argument type and conclusion;
- two response strategies that differ in argumentative task or central reason,
  not merely in author name;
- a three-row source-role grid using verified, assigned, actually read sources;
- three criteria relevant to the question;
- a strong counterargument with an identified premise, inference, scope claim,
  or comparison as its point of contact;
- at least two matters explicitly reserved for the Weeks 8–10 sources;
- a scheduled next action naming source, task, duration, and block; and
- an honest AI-use declaration.

Do not assign separate points or infer a later A-05 grade from completion.

## Diagnostic prompts

- **Question neutrality:** “Could more than one conclusion still be reached?”
- **Argument type:** “Is the target strict inconsistency, evidence,
  probability, moral evil, natural evil, or another defined form?”
- **Material difference:** “Do these responses do different work, or do they
  restate the same answer in two voices?”
- **Source role:** “Which assigned location supports that attribution?”
- **Criterion fit:** “Why would this criterion change the comparison?”
- **Objection fit:** “Which premise or inference does the objection pressure?”
- **Scope:** “What should remain undecided until Weeks 8–10?”

The facilitator may name the defect but must not select the paper question,
responses, criteria, counterargument, or conclusion. If a source has not been
verified and read, direct the student back to the approved course sources
rather than repairing or inventing a citation.

## AI and authorship boundary

AI may clarify the instructions or critique a genuine student plan against the
completion standard. It may not create the plan's question, source analysis,
comparison criteria, counterargument, judgments, or prose. Require correction
of any declaration that fails to identify the tool, purpose, and affected
portion.
