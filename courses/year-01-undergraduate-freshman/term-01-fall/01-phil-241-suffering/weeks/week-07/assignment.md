# Week 7 Assignment — A-05 Comparative Analysis Planning

## A-05-P01 — Question and Comparison Architecture

**Parent assessment:** A-05 Comparative analysis paper, 20% of the course
grade

**Status:** required one-hour planning milestone; ungraded now and assessed
only as part of the later A-05 submission

**Parent assessment design:** a focused comparison of one argument from evil
and two materially different responses, including a strong counterargument

**Time budget:** 1.00 hour of the approved 10.00-hour A-05 allocation

**Week 7 outcomes prepared:** `LO-02`, `LO-03`. The completed parent A-05
assessment also measures `LO-05` after the later assigned sources are studied.

This milestone builds a question and comparison structure. It does not require
a thesis, polished prose, new research, or a final source choice. Weeks 8–10
will add further material before A-05 is completed.

## Time-boxed process

| Step | Planning action | Time |
|---|---|---:|
| Define the provisional problem | Write one neutral question about an argument from evil; identify whether the target is logical, evidential, moral, natural, or mixed | 10 min |
| Select candidate responses | Name two materially different response strategies and explain in one phrase why they are not duplicates | 10 min |
| Build a source-role grid | For the argument and each response, list a verified assigned source, its principal claim, the student's reconstruction task, and one limitation | 15 min |
| Identify comparison criteria | Select three criteria such as logical force, explanatory scope, necessity, alternatives, amount/distribution, beneficiary, biblical fit, or pastoral limit | 10 min |
| Frame a strong counterargument | State the strongest anticipated pressure on the response currently judged more promising; identify its point of contact | 10 min |
| Plan next work and document AI use | Name what Weeks 8–10 must supply, schedule the next work block, and complete the declaration | 5 min |
|  | **Total** | **60 min** |

## Required planning record

Retain or submit one planning document containing:

1. a **provisional question** written neutrally enough that more than one
   conclusion remains possible;
2. the **argument type** and the strongest conclusion the argument seeks;
3. two **materially different response strategies**, each described in one or
   two sentences;
4. a three-row **source-role grid**:

   | Role | Verified assigned source | Principal claim to reconstruct | Limitation or open question |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Argument from evil |  |  |  |
   | Response 1 |  |  |  |
   | Response 2 |  |  |  |

5. three **comparison criteria** and a sentence explaining why each is
   relevant to the chosen question;
6. one **strong counterargument** directed at a premise, inference, scope
   claim, or comparison used by the response currently judged more promising;
7. a **development note** naming at least two matters that cannot responsibly
   be settled until the assigned Weeks 8–10 sources are studied; and
8. a scheduled **next action** naming source, task, duration, and work block.

A permissible Week 7 plan may use the strict logical argument, Hume's
evidential pressure, the problem of natural evil, or another argument already
assigned. It may also reserve the final argument choice until Week 8 by stating
selection criteria and identifying what the Week 8 sources must establish.
Do not invent a citation or use a source not yet verified and read.

## Authorship and AI-use declaration

The provisional question, source analysis, comparison criteria,
counterargument, and planning judgments must be the student's own. AI may
clarify these instructions or critique a genuine student-created plan against
the published completion standard. It may not choose the argument or
responses, reconstruct source claims, formulate the counterargument, decide
the comparison, or draft/rewrite material for submission.

End the planning record with one declaration:

- `No AI used.`
- `Permitted AI use: [tool], [purpose], [affected portion]. I verified and
  retained responsibility for the submitted planning judgments.`

The milestone is complete according to [rubric.md](rubric.md). It receives no
separate points and does not predetermine the later A-05 grade.
