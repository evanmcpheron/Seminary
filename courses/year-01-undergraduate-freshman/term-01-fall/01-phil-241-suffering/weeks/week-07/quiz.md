# Week 7 Midterm Readiness Check

**Status:** ungraded retrieval practice; not A-02, not A-04, and worth no
course points

**Time limit:** 15 minutes, including correction

Complete items 1–6 in eight minutes without books, notes, web, AI, or help.
Then open the instructor key and spend seven minutes marking corrections. The
questions sample categories and distinctions; they do not predict or disclose
the midterm questions.

## Closed-book retrieval — 8 minutes

1. Which statement best describes a defense?

   - A. It identifies God's known reason for every actual evil.
   - B. It supplies a possibly true model that answers a strict inconsistency
     claim.
   - C. It proves that theism is more probable than every rival view.
   - D. It gives pastoral advice independent of argument.

2. Which claim confuses a possible world with a feasible world?

   - A. Some complete descriptions of reality are incoherent.
   - B. A creature's free act is not identical to an act determined by God.
   - C. If a world containing always-right free choices is conceivable, God
     can actualize it while determining those same choices.
   - D. A consistency defense can have a narrower conclusion than a theodicy.

3. Which statement best preserves Augustine's privation account?

   - A. Injury is unreal because evil is not a substance.
   - B. Evil is an independently created nature opposed to good.
   - C. Evil is a corruption or lack in something that remains good as a
     created nature.
   - D. Privation proves why every loss was permitted.

4. Stump's alternative-means pressure asks:

   - A. whether natural regularity is logically impossible;
   - B. whether the relevant knowledge and goods could be secured through a
     less costly feasible route;
   - C. whether every moral choice is determined; or
   - D. whether evil is a substance.

5. Give one sentence distinguishing Humean evidential pressure from Mackie's
strict logical challenge.

6. Give one sentence explaining why eschatological promise does not by itself
supply a token explanation.
