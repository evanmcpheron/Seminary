# Week 7 Study Guide — Synthesis under Scope Control

required source review and both lectures. It is not a practice midterm and
does not predict the exam questions. Attempt all sections before consulting

## 1. Modal and conclusion-scope sort

For each move, write the strongest warranted label from this list:
**possible**, **feasible**, **necessary**, **probable**, **actual**, **known**.
Then state which additional premise or evidence would be needed to move one
level stronger.

1. A coherent story describes a morally sufficient divine reason for allowing
   significantly free creatures to choose wrongly.
2. A possible world contains free creatures who always choose rightly.
3. A stable natural process sometimes gives agents knowledge of consequences.
4. A person grows in courage after an injury.
5. Romans 8 locates creation's groaning within future hope.

Complete this sentence twice with different endings: “A successful defense
establishes ______, but it does not establish ______.”

## 3. Eschatology and value-relation check

Classify each claim as **compensation**, **outweighing**, **defeat**,
**eschatological promise**, or **token explanation**. More than one category
may be plausible if you name the missing evidence.

1. A later benefit is given to a person in response to a loss.
2. A life as a whole contains more good than evil.
3. An evil is integrally taken into a redeemed whole that benefits the
   sufferer without making the evil good in itself.
4. God promises that death and pain will not characterize renewed creation.
5. This exact loss was necessary to bring this person to final communion.

Write two sentences explaining why Lewis chapter 10 can support claim 4
without establishing claim 5.
