# Week 7 Evaluation Standards

## A-04 Midterm examination, 100 points

### Part I — Concepts and distinctions, 20 points

Each of eight items is worth 2.5 points:

- **2.5:** both terms or categories are accurate, the decisive distinction is
  explicit, and the required limit or implication is correct;
- **2.0:** substantially accurate with one minor omission or imprecision;
- **1.0–1.5:** partial knowledge, but a category is blurred or the stated
  implication exceeds the definition;
- **0–0.5:** absent, materially incorrect, circular, or reverses the
  distinction.

Equivalent precise wording receives full credit. No memorized quotation is
required.

### Grade interpretation

The course scale applies: 93–100 A range, 83–92 B range, 73–82 C range,
60–72 D range, and below 60 F range. Accuracy, charitable representation,
argument quality, biblical context, and conclusion scope—not agreement with a
preferred confessional conclusion—control the score.

Missing or false integrity documentation and prohibited assistance are handled
under course policy rather than silently converted into an ordinary content
deduction. A structured retake may be offered only under the documented
remediation process in the instructor materials; the original result remains
in the course record.

## A-05-P01 planning completion standard

This milestone is ungraded and carries no separate points. It is complete when
the planning record includes:

- a neutral provisional question and identified argument type;
- two materially different candidate response strategies;
- a three-row grid using only verified, assigned, actually read sources;
- three relevant comparison criteria with reasons;
- one strong counterargument with a named point of contact;
- two questions reserved for Weeks 8–10 rather than guessed in advance;
- a scheduled next action with source, task, duration, and work block; and
- an accurate AI-use declaration.

An incomplete element is returned for completion. The facilitator may identify
a missing category or weak comparison but may not choose the paper's position,
sources, reasoning, counterargument, or prose for the student.
