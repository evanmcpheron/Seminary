# Week 7 — First-Half Synthesis

## Weekly purpose

Week 7 completes the course's first movement. Lecture 1 reads C. S. Lewis's
*The Problem of Pain* as a cumulative popular Christian theodicy, gives chapter
10's appeal to heaven its full structural force, and then tests that appeal for
necessity, alternatives, proportionality, distribution, beneficiary, value
relation, and token scope. Lecture 2 synthesizes Augustine, Aquinas, Leibniz,
Hume's dialogue, Mackie, Plantinga, Swinburne, Stump, Lewis, and the completed
biblical comparison by the distinct questions and argumentative tasks they
address.

The week's graded work is the approved A-04 closed-book, timed, three-hour
midterm. Its student-facing questions and scoring standards are separated from
the instructor-only answer standards. A one-hour ungraded A-05 planning
milestone begins the later comparative paper without predetermining its
question, sources, or conclusion.
