## A-02-Q03 — Retrieval Quiz 3, 20 points

- Part I: eight multiple-choice items at 1 point each, 8 points total.
- Part II: two short analysis items at 6 points each, 12 points total.
- Item 9: accurate Swinburne reconstruction, 2 points; accurate Stump objection
  and point of contact, 2 points; warranted/unwarranted scope, 2 points.
- Item 10: three distinct errors, 1 point each; accurate use of *token
  explanation* and *defeat*, 2 points; responsible action, 1 point.
- Minor wording differences receive full credit when the philosophical content
  is equivalent. The separate instructor key controls scoring.
- Missing or false integrity documentation is handled under course policy,
  not silently converted into an ordinary content deduction.

Score interpretation follows the course scale: 18.6–20.0 A range, 16.6–18.5 B
range, 14.6–16.5 C range, 12.0–14.5 D range, and below 12.0 F range.

## A-04-P02 — Midterm preparation completion standard

This Week 6 milestone carries no separate points and must not be averaged into
the A-04 midterm score. It is complete when the preparation record contains:

- the status of all three A-04-P01 remediation actions;
- eighteen definitions attempted closed-book and visibly corrected;
- accurate entries for the six required Week 6 terms;
- three numbered argument maps first produced closed-book, including the three
  required time-period/source groupings;
- an eight-row source-voice matrix with source claim, target, and limitation;
- a mixed-case analysis that distinguishes general, kind-level, and token
  providence claims and names an immediate care duty;
- at least six visible corrections in the four required categories;
- a ranked, scheduled three-item Week 7 remediation plan; and
- an honest record of any permitted assistance.

Incomplete elements are returned for completion. The facilitator may identify
a missing distinction, voice, or source location but may not write the
student's definitions, maps, case analysis, or later exam answer.

## Integrity control

Fabricated citations, concealed prohibited AI authorship, quiz assistance, or
assistance during the closed-book A-04 retrieval phase are handled under course
policy rather than quietly repaired through scoring. Evaluation concerns
accurate source representation, argument structure, biblical context, scope,
and pastoral responsibility—not agreement with the curriculum's confessional
conclusion.
