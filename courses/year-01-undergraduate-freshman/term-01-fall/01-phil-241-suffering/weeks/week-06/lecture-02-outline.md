# Lecture 2 Outline — Value, Defeat, and the Limits of Providence Claims

## Objectives

- Distinguish prevention, permission, outweighing, compensation, and defeat.
- Explain Chisholm's contrast between balancing off and defeating evil.
- Test greater-good proposals for necessity, proportion, distribution,
  beneficiary, agency, and epistemic access.
- Evaluate what Lewis's chapter on hell adds to questions of judgment and
  freedom, and what it leaves unresolved.
- State the difference between general providence and a case-specific divine
  reason.
- Use Job and Romans 8 to discipline both skeptical and overconfident claims.
- Apply trauma-aware boundaries to philosophical explanation.
