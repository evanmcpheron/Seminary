## 1. Causal classification

For each stipulated case, classify the immediate harm as **moral**, **natural**,
or **mixed**. Identify any responsible agent, evidence still needed, and one
appropriate prevention or care action.

1. A person intentionally contaminates another person's water.
2. A wild animal is injured by a naturally falling tree in an area with no
   human involvement.
3. A storm damages a building after its owner knowingly ignored a required
   repair.
4. A previously unknown disease process causes illness, and an official later
   conceals evidence needed to contain it.

Write two sentences explaining why *natural cause* does not mean *unimportant*
and why *mixed case* must not be used to blame a sufferer.

## 3. Lewis speculation audit

Classify each as **argument**, **hypothesis**, **illustration**, **theological
claim**, or **admitted limit**. More than one label may apply if explained.

1. Some animal sensation may lack a temporally unified self.
2. A nonhuman rebellious power may have corrupted animal life before humans.
3. Human dominion creates responsibility toward animals.
4. A tame animal's identity may be restored through its relation to humans.
5. The relational illustration does not cover wild or mistreated animals.
6. Human beings do not know enough to give a settled account of animal pain.

Choose one item and state what evidence would be needed before it could
function as an actual explanation. Do not research beyond the assigned sources.
