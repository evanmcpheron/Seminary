## Part II — 12 points

### Item 9 — 6 points

- **Swinburne reconstruction, 2 points:** award full credit for a coherent
  sequence linking responsible choice to knowledge of consequences, that
  knowledge to regular natural processes and observed harms, and the resulting
  opportunities to a possible reason for divine permission.
- **Stump objection and point of contact, 2 points:** the availability of one
  learning route does not show it is necessary; less costly acquisition may be
  possible. The objection targets the no-alternative or necessity line, not the
  bare claim that suffering can teach.
- **Scope, 2 points:** accept limits such as “not every natural evil is
  justified,” “the actual amount and distribution are not explained,” or “no
  token divine reason follows.”

Do not require the exact order or wording above. An answer that describes only
higher-order goods can earn at most 1 of the first 2 points unless it connects
them to significant choice.

### Item 10 — 6 points

Award 1 point for each of three distinct errors:

- a general biblical/providence claim is converted into a token explanation;
- the text does not identify caretaker improvement as the purpose;
- possible later good is confused with necessity;
- benefit to the caretaker ignores the animal as cost-bearer;
- future redemption is confused with present completed defeat; or
- technical defeat requires organic integration, not merely later benefit.

Award 2 points for accurate terminology: a *token explanation* assigns a
reason to this particular event, and *defeat* requires an organic value
relation not established by citing Romans 8 or later improvement. Award 1 point
for an immediate responsible action such as qualified animal care, stabilizing
the structure, safety review, evidence preservation, or appropriate reporting.
