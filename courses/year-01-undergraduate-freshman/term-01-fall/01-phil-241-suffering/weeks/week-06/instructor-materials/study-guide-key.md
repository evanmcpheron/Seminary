# Instructor Key — Week 6 Study Guide

Keep this file separate until the student has attempted every section. It
supports self-correction of ungraded practice and is not a model response for
A-01-J06, A-02-Q03, or the midterm.

## 1. Causal classification

1. **Moral:** the intentional contamination is culpable human action. Relevant
   actions include immediate safety and medical response, preserving evidence,
   reporting under applicable rules, and preventing further access. The
   exercise does not determine legal guilt.
2. **Natural:** on the stipulated facts, no human choice caused the fall or
   injury. Appropriate action includes safe animal assessment and qualified
   care. More evidence could revise the classification.
3. **Mixed:** the storm is a natural process; the knowing failure to repair is
   a potentially culpable omission contributing to harm. Evidence about notice,
   responsibility, feasibility, and causation is still needed.
4. **Mixed sequence:** the initial disease is natural on the facts; concealment
   is moral evil if knowing and culpable and may increase later harm. A strong
   answer distinguishes initial cause from subsequent aggravation.

*Natural* identifies an immediate causal category, not moral insignificance.
*Mixed* distributes analysis among contributing causes; it does not assign
fault to the person harmed.

## 2. Swinburne–Stump reconstruction

Accept equivalent, source-controlled maps. A complete reconstruction includes:

- significant moral responsibility as the good;
- knowledge of the consequences of options as a condition of responsible
  choice;
- regular processes as a means of learning by induction;
- actual harms as evidence of what agents can cause, allow, prevent, or relieve;
- a limited conclusion that this connection may supply a reason to permit some
  natural evil; and
- explicit additional premises about necessity, alternatives, proportion, and
  distribution before a comprehensive theodicy follows.

Stump's necessity objection targets the move from “one route to knowledge” to
“no less costly route is available.” Her better-means objection asks whether
the distinctive goods of discovery and independent inquiry justify the total
cost compared with alternatives. A strong limited verdict says Swinburne
identifies real goods but has not thereby justified every actual natural evil.

Do not require the student's map to reproduce these sentences. Locations must
come from Peterson chapters 25–26 in the student's lawful edition.

## 3. Lewis speculation audit

1. **Argument and hypothesis:** a philosophy-of-mind proposal; not an
   established scientific finding in this course.
2. **Hypothesis and theological speculation:** a proposed causal extension of
   creaturely freedom; not a demonstrated history.
3. **Theological claim:** used constructively to ground human responsibility,
   not to explain all animal pain.
4. **Illustration and eschatological speculation:** explicitly limited in
   scope.
5. **Admitted limit:** prevents the illustration from becoming comprehensive.
6. **Admitted limit:** controls the warranted confidence of the chapter.

Evidence needs depend on the selected item: a consciousness claim needs
relevant philosophical and empirical support; a causal history needs positive
historical or theological warrant; an eschatological proposal needs sound
exegetical and systematic argument. Naming mere possibility is insufficient
for an actual explanation.

## 4. Value and providence grid

Accept varied non-traumatic examples if they preserve these distinctions:

- **Prevention:** stopping an evil; does not show why other evils are permitted.
- **Permission:** not preventing; does not entail causation, intention,
  approval, or justification.
- **Outweighing:** independent goods make the whole positive despite evil; does
  not show organic connection or goodness for the sufferer.
- **Compensation:** responsive benefit to the harmed party; does not make the
  evil necessary or the original agent justified.
- **Defeat:** organic integration into a distinctive good whole; does not show
  that any actual evil is defeated or that a wrongdoer is excused.

Providence levels:

1. **General doctrine.**
2. **Kind-level explanation.**
3. **Token explanation.** Evidence connecting this event to this specified
   purpose is missing.
4. **General doctrinal/eschatological claim.** Accept *kind-level* only with a
   clear explanation of the intended classification.
5. **Universal collection of token explanations:** it claims a reason for each
   instance and needs evidence of necessity, quantity, distribution, and
   beneficiary.

Job affirms divine rule while rejecting the friends' confident guilt inference;
Romans 8 joins real groaning to future liberation without mapping each harm to
its purpose. Neither text supplies the missing token evidence.
