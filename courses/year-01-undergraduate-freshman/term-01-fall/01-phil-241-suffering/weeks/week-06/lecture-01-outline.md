# Lecture 1 Outline — Natural Evil, Animal Suffering, and Moral Choice

## Objectives

- Distinguish moral, natural, and mixed causal cases.
- Explain why animal suffering pressures human-centered free-will accounts.
- Reconstruct Swinburne's knowledge-and-choice proposal.
- State Stump's necessity and better-means objections accurately.
- Evaluate stable-order, higher-order-good, fall, and nonhuman-agency accounts
  by the exact explanatory burden each assumes.
- Read Lewis's proposals as explicitly speculative rather than established.
- Relate Job and Romans 8 to providence, creaturely limits, lament, and hope.
