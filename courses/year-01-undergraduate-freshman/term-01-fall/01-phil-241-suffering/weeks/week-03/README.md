# Week 3 — Historical Approaches

## Weekly purpose

Week 3 compares four influential approaches to evil. Augustine and Aquinas
clarify what evil is and how a good creation can be corrupted; Leibniz argues
from divine wisdom and whole-world value; Hume's dialogue tests what a mixed
world permits an observer to infer about divine goodness. Lewis's accounts of
human wickedness and the fall provide a modern Christian conversation partner.

The week distinguishes ontology, causation, compatibility, and evidence. It
also tests every historical proposal against the Week 2 canonical pattern
without treating a philosophical account as knowledge of God's reason for a
particular person's suffering.
