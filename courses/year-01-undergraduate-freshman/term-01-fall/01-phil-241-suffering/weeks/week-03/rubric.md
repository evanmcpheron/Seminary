# Week 3 Evaluation Standards

## A-01-J03 — Reading and Argument Journal 3, 10 points

| Criterion | Outcome | Excellent | Proficient | Developing | Deficient | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Historical claim and reasons | LO-01, LO-03 | States a precise claim and reconstructs at least two source-controlled reasons or distinctions. | Claim and reasons are substantially accurate with a minor omission. | Claim is too broad or reasons are incomplete or loosely connected. | Misrepresents the source or supplies little relevant reasoning. | 3 |
| Attribution and question control | LO-01, LO-05 | Identifies the dialogue speaker when applicable and clearly distinguishes ontology, cause, compatibility, or evidence. | Attribution and question type are mostly controlled with one minor imprecision. | Conflates question types or uses ambiguous attribution. | Falsely attributes a claim or treats different questions as interchangeable. | 2 |
| Charitable objection | LO-03, LO-05 | Presses a serious objection against the actual reasoning without caricature. | Relevant and fair but briefly developed. | Objection is vague, weak, or partly misdirected. | Attacks motives, caricatures the position, or offers no genuine pressure. | 2 |
| Canonical comparison and judgment | LO-03, LO-04 | Distinguishes biblical claim from inference, makes a relevant comparison, and gives a balanced provisional judgment. | Addresses comparison and judgment with a minor limitation. | Proof is thin, one required distinction is blurred, or judgment is one-sided. | Proof-texts, claims a hidden divine purpose, or omits the comparison or judgment. | 2 |
| Source location, clarity, and declaration | LO-01 | Both sources are locatable; prose is concise; metadata and AI declaration are complete. | One minor citation, clarity, metadata, or declaration defect. | A source is difficult to locate or defects impede review. | Fabricated reference, missing declaration, or prose not assessable as the student's own. | 1 |
|  |  |  |  |  | **Total** | **10** |

Score interpretation follows the course scale: 9.3–10.0 A range, 8.3–9.2 B
range, 7.3–8.2 C range, 6.0–7.2 D range, and below 6.0 F range.

## A-03-D02 — Development milestone completion standard

This Week 3 milestone receives no separate points and must not be averaged into
the final A-03 score. It is complete when the process document contains:

- all ten required headings;
- visible revision from the Week 2 diagnostic skeleton;
- accurate identification of the dialogue speaker, target, and locatable
  assigned-edition evidence;
- a numbered 5–7-premise map with a conclusion and any reconstructed additions
  clearly labeled;
- a correct distinction between compatibility and evidential support;
- one ambiguity, one suppressed premise, and one serious source-relevant
  pressure;
- a fair Augustine, Aquinas, or Leibniz reply whose exact point of contact is
  identified;
- two questions reserved for final completion rather than guessed answers;
- only sources actually consulted; and
- a complete AI-use declaration.

Incomplete elements are returned for completion. Instructor feedback should
identify source, structure, or comparison problems without constructing the
student's final argument.

## Ungraded retrieval practice

The Week 3 retrieval practice carries no points and is not an A-02 assessment.
Completion means the student attempted the first phase without sources, checked
the answers against assigned materials, and recorded at least one correction.
The self-correction record is for learning and does not enter the grade.

## Integrity control

Fabricated citations, concealed prohibited AI authorship, or other integrity
concerns are handled under the course policy rather than quietly repaired
through scoring. Evaluation concerns accurate representation, reasoning,
responsible biblical comparison, and charitable judgment—not assent to the
curriculum's confessional conclusions.
