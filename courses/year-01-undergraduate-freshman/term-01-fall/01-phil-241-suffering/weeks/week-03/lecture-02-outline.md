# Lecture 2 Outline — Leibniz and Hume on Worlds, Evidence, and Divine Goodness

## Objectives

- Reconstruct Leibniz's objection, denied premise, and best-world reply.
- Explain possible worlds, compossibility, contingency, and whole-world value.
- Reconstruct Philo's empirical challenge from mixed phenomena.
- Distinguish logical compatibility from evidential support.
- Compare the rival arguments charitably before confessional evaluation.
