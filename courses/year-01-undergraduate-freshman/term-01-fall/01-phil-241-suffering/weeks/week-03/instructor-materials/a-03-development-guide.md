# Instructor Development Guide — A-03-D02

## Status and aim

A-03-D02 is a required, unscored process milestone. It should show the student
replacing Week 2's diagnostic skeleton with a locatable reconstruction of
Philo's reasoning in the assigned Hume selection. Do not grade it as a finished
brief or preview the Week 4 completion prompt.

## Completion review

Mark the milestone complete only when it meets every item in the public
completion standard. In particular, check that:

- the student's change log preserves evidence of revision;
- dialogue speaker and immediate argumentative target are named;
- locations point to the student's acquired assigned edition;
- 5–7 numbered premises lead intelligibly to a conclusion;
- any premise absent from the source is labeled as a reconstruction;
- compatibility is separated from positive evidential support;
- the objection reaches the map rather than merely repeating disagreement;
- the Christian reply contacts a specified premise, inference, or question;
  and
- the AI-use declaration is complete.

## Accurate range and common errors

Reasonable maps may differ in granularity. A defensible reconstruction will
normally connect mixed observed effects, limits on inferring qualities beyond
effects, and the resulting lack of empirical warrant for unlimited goodness.
Some students will use the four circumstances as support for the mixed-effects
premise; others will give one circumstance its own premise. Either structure
can be acceptable if the source and inferential role are clear.

Return for revision if the student:

- attributes the anthology's editorial title as a quotation from Hume;
- converts Philo's evidential pressure into a bare logical contradiction;
- assumes that a possible divine reason supplies observational evidence for
  perfect goodness;
- reports a historical reply without showing which claim it addresses; or
- silently replaces the student's D01 work with an externally generated map.

## Feedback stems

Use prompts that preserve student authorship:

- “Which speaker advances premise __, and where?”
- “Does this line assert compatibility or evidential support?”
- “What unstated rule moves you from premise __ to the conclusion?”
- “Which exact premise does this historical reply challenge?”
- “What changed from D01, and what evidence caused the change?”

Do not provide a corrected premise sequence, supply the suppressed premise, or
write the historical reply. If incomplete, identify the relevant standard and
return the milestone for the student's own revision.
