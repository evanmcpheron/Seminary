# Week 3 Assignments

This file contains the graded A-01 journal entry and the required A-03
development milestone. Complete both only after the assigned readings and the

## A-01-J03 — Reading and Argument Journal 3

**Parent assessment:** A-01 Reading and argument journals

**Status:** graded, 10 points within the twelve-entry A-01 series

**Time budget:** 0.50 hour

**Outcomes:** `LO-01`, `LO-03`, `LO-04`, `LO-05`

### Purpose

Accurately reconstruct one historical claim, expose it to serious pressure,
and compare it with the Week 2 canonical grammar without collapsing historical
and biblical sources into one voice.

### Task

Choose **one** assigned selection by Augustine, Aquinas, Leibniz, or Hume in
Peterson's anthology. Write **300–400 words** under five headings:

1. **Claim:** State one focused claim from the selection. For Hume, name the
   dialogue speaker and avoid treating a character's speech as uncomplicated
   authorial assertion.
2. **Reasons:** Reconstruct at least two reasons or distinctions that support
   the claim. Indicate whether the source is addressing evil's nature, causal
   origin, compatibility with God, or evidential force.
3. **Strong objection:** Present the strongest relevant objection you can state
   within the space. Target the reasoning rather than the thinker's motives or
   character.
4. **Canonical comparison:** Use one assigned Week 2 biblical passage to state
   a point of resonance, qualification, or tension. Distinguish what the
   biblical text says from your theological inference.
5. **Provisional judgment:** State what the historical proposal explains well
   and one question it leaves unresolved.

Use paraphrase. A direct quotation is not required. If you quote, keep it brief
and cite the location precisely. Do not claim that the selected thinker knew
God's reason for a particular person's suffering.

## A-03-D02 — Argument Reconstruction Brief Development 2

**Parent assessment:** A-03 Argument reconstruction brief

**Status:** required development milestone; no separate Week 3 points

**Time budget:** 2.00 hours of the approved five-hour A-03 budget

**Outcomes developed this week:** `LO-01`, `LO-03`, `LO-05`

This milestone replaces the Week 2 diagnostic skeleton with a source-controlled
map of Philo's reasoning in the assigned Hume selection. It is not the final
brief. Do not use a later internet summary or anticipate the Week 4 completion
instructions.

### Time-boxed process

| Step | Deliverable | Time |
|---|---|---:|
| Reopen process evidence | Review A-03-D01 and copy forward only the term definitions and gaps that remain relevant | 0.17 h |

| Reconstruct | Write a numbered 5–7-premise argument and conclusion in your own words; add one subargument only if a premise needs support | 0.58 h |

### Required headings

1. Change log from A-03-D01
2. Speaker, target, and source locations
3. Numbered premise-conclusion map
4. Subargument, if needed

6. Ambiguity, suppressed premise, and strongest pressure

### Source-control requirements

- Work from the assigned Hume selection in Peterson; record the acquired
  edition and its page or licensed digital locations.
- Attribute speeches to Philo, Cleanthes, or Demea before making a qualified
  claim about Hume's larger dialogue.
- Do not present the anthology's editorial title as a sentence spoken by Hume.
- Mark added premises as reconstructions rather than quotations.
- Represent the selected Christian reply on its own terms. A privation account,
  causal account, and whole-world defense do not automatically answer the same
  question.
- Preserve the D01 and D02 files so the instructor can see genuine revision.

Submit a concise process document, not polished final prose. Completion is
defined in [rubric.md](rubric.md).

The premise map, source attribution, objection, comparison, and revision

critique a map the student has already written. It may not construct, repair,
formalize, or rewrite the submitted map or historical reply. Record any
permitted assistance, including the tool, request, and portion affected; if
none was used, state “No AI used.”
