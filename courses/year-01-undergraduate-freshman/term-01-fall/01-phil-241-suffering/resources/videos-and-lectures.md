# Video and Lecture Research

## Comparative perspective candidate

- *Eastern Traditions: Why Are Suffering and Ritual Vital?* Closer To Truth,
  hosted by Robert Lawrence Kuhn, published 2024-04-29, 26:47. The official page
  provides the video and a complete transcript without charge. Contributors are
  Helen De Cruz, Jay Garfield, Swami Medhananda, Franklin Perkins, and Yang
  Xiao. [Source record](source-records/closer-to-truth-eastern-traditions.source.yaml)

  Possible viewing-guide questions for later design:

  1. Which assumptions about personhood, justice, karma, rebirth, or cosmic
     order shape the contributors' accounts of suffering?
  2. Why does suffering generate different intellectual pressures in these
     traditions than it does under classical Christian theism?
  3. Which comparisons clarify the Christian problem of evil, and which risk
     flattening important differences among traditions?

This record supports charitable comparative research. It is not a weekly media
assignment or an endorsement of every view represented.
