# Recommended-Reading Research

These verified works are candidates for Prompt 05. Inclusion here does not make
them assigned reading.

## Books

- Alvin Plantinga, *God, Freedom, and Evil*. Eerdmans, 1989. A concise primary
  presentation of the free-will defense; USD 16.99 in paperback or ebook on the
  publisher page as of 2026-09-04.
  [Source record](source-records/plantinga-god-freedom-evil.source.yaml)
- Rudolf Otto, *The Idea of the Holy*, trans. John W. Harvey. Oxford University
  Press, 1958 reprint. This is a research candidate for the catalog's reference
  to “Rudolph Otto”; the catalog does not identify Otto's exact work.
  [Source record](source-records/otto-idea-of-the-holy.source.yaml)
- Marilyn McCord Adams, *Horrendous Evils and the Goodness of God*. Cornell
  University Press paperback, 2000. A sustained treatment of horrendous evil
  and defeat; current publisher-direct price was not displayed.
  [Source record](source-records/adams-horrendous-evils.source.yaml)
- Eleonore Stump, *Wandering in Darkness: Narrative and the Problem of
  Suffering*. Oxford University Press, 2010. Useful for second-personal and
  narrative approaches; publisher price was not displayed.
  [Source record](source-records/stump-wandering-in-darkness.source.yaml)
- John Hick, *Evil and the God of Love*. Palgrave Macmillan, 2010 reissue. A
  major soul-making theodicy; the publisher listed USD 84.99, so anthology or
  library access is preferable.
  [Source record](source-records/hick-evil-and-the-god-of-love.source.yaml)
- John S. Feinberg, *The Many Faces of Evil*, rev. and expanded ed. Crossway,
  2005. A broadly evangelical theological-system comparison; USD 40.00 on the
  publisher page as of 2026-09-04.
  [Source record](source-records/feinberg-many-faces-of-evil.source.yaml)
- N. T. Wright, *Evil and the Justice of God*. IVP, 2013. A biblical-theological
  complement; USD 24.99 on the publisher page as of 2026-09-04.
  [Source record](source-records/wright-evil-and-the-justice-of-god.source.yaml)

## Articles and reference works

- J. L. Mackie, “Evil and Omnipotence.” The original article is paywalled, but
  the Peterson anthology supplies a lower-cost route to Mackie's position.
  [Source record](source-records/mackie-evil-and-omnipotence.source.yaml)
- William L. Rowe, “The Problem of Evil and Some Varieties of Atheism.” A free
  deposited copy is indexed through PhilPapers/PhilArchive.
  [Source record](source-records/rowe-problem-of-evil.source.yaml)
- Roderick M. Chisholm, “The Defeat of Good and Evil.” Library or licensed
  database access is the prudent route; no open full text was verified.
  [Source record](source-records/chisholm-defeat-of-good-and-evil.source.yaml)
- Stephen J. Wykstra, “The Humean Obstacle to Evidential Arguments from
  Suffering.” Library access is preferred; the free SEP entry on skeptical
  theism supplies orientation, not a replacement for the article.
  [Source record](source-records/wykstra-humean-obstacle.source.yaml)
- Paul Draper, “Pain and Pleasure: An Evidential Problem for Theists.” The
  Peterson anthology is the lower-cost access route identified in this review.
  [Source record](source-records/draper-pain-and-pleasure.source.yaml)
- Marilyn McCord Adams and Stewart Sutherland, “Horrendous Evils and the
  Goodness of God.” A free author-deposited version is indexed by PhilPapers.
  [Source record](source-records/adams-sutherland-horrendous-evils.source.yaml)
- James R. Beebe, “Logical Problem of Evil,” *Internet Encyclopedia of
  Philosophy*. Free orientation to the logical problem and major responses.
  [Source record](source-records/beebe-logical-problem-of-evil.source.yaml)
- Nick Trakakis, “The Evidential Problem of Evil,” *Internet Encyclopedia of
  Philosophy*. Free orientation to evidential formulations and replies.
  [Source record](source-records/trakakis-evidential-problem-of-evil.source.yaml)
- Timothy Perrine, “Skeptical Theism,” *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
  Free, stable Spring 2023 archive edition.
  [Source record](source-records/perrine-skeptical-theism.source.yaml)

Selection and excerpt pagination remain Prompt 05 decisions.
