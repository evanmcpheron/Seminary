# Learning Outcomes

By the end of PHIL 241, the student will be able to do the following.

| ID | Measurable outcome | Primary cognitive level | Direct assessments |
|---|---|---|---|
| LO-01 | Distinguish and correctly use the central concepts, argument forms, and methodological categories in philosophical and theological treatments of suffering and evil. | Analyze | A-01, A-02, A-03, A-04, A-07 |
| LO-02 | Reconstruct and evaluate major logical and evidential arguments from evil in premise-conclusion form without caricaturing their proponents. | Evaluate | A-02, A-03, A-04, A-05, A-06, A-07 |
| LO-03 | Compare leading Christian defenses and theodicies, including their claims, limits, and principal objections. | Evaluate | A-01, A-02, A-04, A-05, A-06, A-07 |
| LO-04 | Interpret representative biblical texts and canonical themes concerning suffering, evil, lament, providence, judgment, and hope using the ESV and NASB 2020 responsibly. | Analyze | A-01, A-02, A-04, A-07 |
| LO-05 | Present major non-Christian, skeptical, and intra-Christian perspectives accurately and charitably before offering confessional evaluation. | Evaluate | A-01, A-05, A-06, A-07 |
| LO-06 | Formulate and defend a coherent broadly evangelical Protestant response to the problem of evil that integrates philosophical reasoning, Scripture, and historical theology while acknowledging unresolved objections. | Create | A-06, A-07 |
| LO-07 | Differentiate philosophical explanation from pastoral response and apply trauma-aware, non-coercive, and appropriately bounded judgment to a case involving suffering. | Apply | A-01, A-06, A-07 |

## Outcome conventions

- `LO-01` supplies the vocabulary and analytic distinctions presupposed by the
  later outcomes.
- `LO-02` through `LO-05` require evidence-based analysis and charitable
  representation before evaluation.
- `LO-06` is constructive but does not permit the student to ignore live
  objections, overstate consensus, or identify the course's synthesis with
  official Wheaton College teaching.
- `LO-07` assesses disciplined judgment, not counseling competence. The student
  is not asked to diagnose, provide therapy, or disclose personal trauma.

The assessment codes and measurement points are defined in
[assessment-map.md](assessment-map.md). Every outcome has at least two direct
measures, and the cumulative final examination measures all seven.
