# Course Bibliography

All adopted and recommended items below have verified source records created by
Prompt 04. The access label is a Prompt 05 design decision. Prices are not part
of the course requirement because they change; current access notes remain in
the linked records. Students must obtain lawful purchased, library,
publisher-hosted, or licensed access.

## Selection and pagination policy

The schedule assigns the verified Peterson edition by named author or source
group rather than supplying unverified page ranges. Later week guides must check
the student's acquired print or digital edition before adding exact pagination.
Where a standalone article is also listed above, the bibliography does not
require a second purchase when the assigned text is already present in Peterson.

The Wheaton catalog and Yale comparable-course model informed identity and design
research only; neither is represented as an assigned course reading. Their
verified records remain under `resources/source-records/`.
